Nashville Feasibility Studies

Davidson, Williamson, Sumner and Rutherford are four of the fourteen counties in the Census Bureau's Nashville metro area. On September 5, 2025 the City of Spring Hill published “Projects with Vested Rights Under Sewer Moratorium”, a list tied to its consent order with the Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation 10. The Census Bureau's July 1, 2025 estimate for Davidson County, the core, is 745,904 residents 1.

Growth

Permits and residents in Davidson County, then the households and older residents of the fourteen-county census metro.

Davidson County authorized 5,632 new private housing units in 2025 1. In the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 estimates the county held 745,904 residents on July 1, 2025, against an estimates base of 715,887 on April 1, 2020, a change QuickFacts gives as 4.2% 1. For the same July date it gives 721,074 for Nashville-Davidson (balance), a city geography inside the county, and a change of 4.6% from April 2020 2.

In the 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate, the Census Bureau's Nashville-Davidson, Murfreesboro and Franklin metro area held 829,134 households, with a median household income of $85,447 and a median value for owner-occupied homes of $409,600 12. The same 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate counts 292,294 residents aged 65 and over 12. Those four readings cover all fourteen of the metro area's counties: Cannon, Cheatham, Davidson, Dickson, Hickman, Macon, Maury, Robertson, Rutherford, Smith, Sumner, Trousdale, Williamson and Wilson.

Economic Base

State, city and university headcounts, the MSA's payroll count.

The Cauble Group's 2026 list puts the State of Tennessee at 27,000, Nissan North America at about 11,000 across Tennessee, Metro Nashville Government at 10,700 and Vanderbilt University at 9,500 4. Payroll jobs across the MSA numbered 1,192.5 thousand in August 2025 in the Bureau of Labor Statistics count, and 1,200.3 thousand in August 2026 on its preliminary reading 3. The BLS table puts the change at 7.8 thousand jobs, or 0.7%, and does not rate it as statistically significant 3.

Our Northwest Arkansas page, once published, will set out Fayetteville's siting standards for data centers and a data center moratorium in Madison County, Arkansas.

Matthews' Q3 2025 hospitality report cites a $2.1 billion Nissan Stadium project and a $3 billion upgrade at BNA, the city's airport, and gives neither project a status or an opening date 5; both reach this record through that hotel report alone. Institutional Property Advisors' 1Q 2026 multifamily report adds a $1.2 billion Oracle campus, also without a status, and a second Amazon tower at Nashville Yards, for which the research found no value 18.

Supply by Asset Class

Matthews and MMG define the apartment pipeline differently; Matthews and Avison Young cover the rest.

Multifamily: where Nashville's apartments are being built

Matthews' Q2 2026 report counted 11,486 units at an effective rent of $1,612 7; MMG Real Estate Advisors, for the same quarter, counted 11,724 units at an effective rent of $1,586, with stabilized occupancy of 92.3% 6. Matthews also puts vacancy at 5.40% in Q2 2026, against 6.21% in Q1 7; MMG's 92.3% for the same quarter is a stabilized occupancy 6, a different measure, and the two are given with their publishers rather than turned into one figure. Northmarq's Q1 2026 vacancy reading for Downtown Nashville alone is 9.4% 16.

Matthews ranks its submarkets by the pipeline set against the existing stock, and on that measure its Q2 2026 report names Franklin and Brentwood, where the pipeline equals 12.5% of inventory, and Hermitage, Mount Juliet and Lebanon, at 10.1% 7.

Our Huntsville page will place that metro's apartment pipeline by property; Nashville's record places its pipeline by submarket instead. Our Raleigh-Durham page reads Yardi Matrix's Raleigh market by stabilized occupancy and deliveries.

The fourteen-county metro area's median gross rent, taken over renter-occupied units paying cash rent of every age and size, was $1,528 in the 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate 12; it describes the whole rented stock over five years, not a rent for new buildings. Our multifamily feasibility page covers how a new building's lease-up is tested.

Hotels and motels other than casino hotels gave work to 13,873 employees in 2023 across the fourteen-county metro area, at 423 establishments in 2023 (County Business Patterns) 11. Those are counts of establishments, meaning locations with paid employees, and their staff across all fourteen counties, not a count of rooms.

Matthews on industrial space

In Q2 2026, by Matthews' count, Nashville had 9.2 million square feet of industrial space under construction, 8.0 million of it in the submarket Matthews calls East / I-40 and I-24, and Matthews reports more than 90% of the space under construction still available for lease 8. For the same quarter it gives deliveries of 2.5 million square feet, net absorption of 137,000 square feet, vacancy of 7.30% across the market and 8.8% in East / I-40 and I-24, and asking rent averaging $11.70 per square foot 8.

Avison Young on retail space under construction

The research found the vacancy reading in Avison Young's Q1 2026 Nashville report ambiguous, so this page carries only the report's construction figures: 599k SF of retail space under construction, a pipeline 33% smaller than a year earlier 9, the openings a retail study sets its trade area against.

Local Rules

Spring Hill's vested-rights list of September 5, 2025, tied to a state consent order.

The City of Spring Hill's notice of September 5, 2025, titled “Projects with Vested Rights Under Sewer Moratorium,” lists the projects holding vested rights and ties the list to the city's consent order with the Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation 10. Its title names a sewer moratorium, but no primary source in this record sets out the moratorium's own terms, so this page states none. The record also does not say how a project absent from the list is treated.

Hazards

DR-4898, declared in February 2026, opened with Public Assistance in fourteen of the state's counties, Davidson, Williamson and Sumner among them.

FEMA's press release of February 6, 2026 on major disaster declaration DR-4898, for the severe winter storm of late January 2026, made Public Assistance available at first in 14 counties of the state, with Davidson, Williamson and Sumner among them 17. The release names counties, and this record takes no damage figure from it for any of the three. On April 10, 2026, FEMA's Amendment 3 to the declaration added Individual Assistance in further counties, Davidson and Williamson among them 19.

Lenders

Nashville hosts the SBA district office for all of Tennessee; Memphis has a virtual office for West Tennessee counties outside the Nashville metro.

The SBA places its Tennessee District Office on International Plaza Drive in Nashville, names David Glasgow as District Director, and gives the office's territory as the whole state, with a virtual office in Memphis for West Tennessee counties: Carroll, Crockett, Dyer, Fayette, Hardeman, Haywood, Lauderdale, Madison, Shelby and Tipton 15. None of those is among the Nashville metro area's fourteen counties. SBA may require a feasibility study under 13 CFR 120.160(b): SBA may require professional appraisals, a survey, or a feasibility study 13; the program's operating procedures are SOP 50 10 8.1 with Technical Policy Updates 14.

Worked Cases

A Wilson County medical office case and a Blount County cabin resort case.

One published case names a site in the fourteen counties of the Nashville metro area: the Lebanon medical office case tests whether a medical office building with an ambulatory surgery center, on a generic site in Lebanon in Wilson County, can carry SBA 504 debt. The cabin resort case, set in unincorporated Blount County a few miles outside Townsend, tests whether new two-bedroom A-frames can carry SBA 504 debt while the two data vendors that track Townsend's rental market disagree on supply direction and occupancy, and a resort that broke ground in Townsend in September 2026 is due to open in 2027.

Nashville Questions

Apartment counts, the stadium, industrial space, asked and answered.

How many apartments are under construction in Nashville?

Matthews' Q2 2026 report counts 11,486 7, and MMG Real Estate Advisors' report for the same quarter 11,724 6. Their effective rents for the quarter were $1,612 (Matthews) 7 and $1,586 (MMG) 6.

Does the Nissan Stadium project give a Nashville hotel study dated event nights?

Not from this record. Matthews' Q3 2025 hospitality report cites a $2.1 billion Nissan Stadium project and gives it neither a status nor an opening date 5, and no other source on this page mentions it.

What did Matthews report for Nashville industrial space in Q2 2026?

Three readings for the same quarter: 9.2 million square feet under construction, 137,000 square feet of net absorption and asking rent averaging $11.70 per square foot 8. Matthews places most of the space under construction in the submarket it calls East / I-40 and I-24 8.

Nashville's apartments under construction were counted at 11,724 and 11,486 units in 2026, by publishers using different definitions.

A Nashville supply test names the count it uses.
