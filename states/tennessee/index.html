The Tennessee Thesis

Tennessee grows by arrival, and 47 sewer moratoria stood in its wastewater record in 2024.

The Tennessee Advisory Commission on Intergovernmental Relations, TACIR, drafted a 2024 report on the state's wastewater systems with data as of May 2024. It counted 47 active sewer moratoria affecting 36 permittees, 57 local governments under TDEC enforcement orders and 53 under financial oversight 26. Where a town is among them, a project's demand can be real and its tap still unavailable.

The arrivals continue. The Census Bureau put the state at 7,315,076 residents on July 1, 2025, against an estimates base of 6,912,319 in April 2020 1, and the State Data Center ranked the year's gain of 63,785 eighth among the states by number and tenth by percentage 2.

Four Tennessee credit channels and the lead entry in each

  • SBA lending statewide Pinnacle Financial Partners, named No. 1 again in October 2025
  • SBA 7(a), Tennessee banks Paragon Bank of Memphis, first by dollars
  • Rural SBA lending APEX Bank by dollars; Live Oak, Rural Lender of the Year
  • Fund Tennessee the second SSBCI round, plan approved by Treasury in February 2023

SBA in Tennessee

How Tennessee's SBA district sorted its FY2025 lender awards, and what ten years of loan files add.

Figures from the Tennessee District Office and from SBA's own loan-level files.

Rules by Asset Class

A sewer rulebook TDEC is still writing, a Certificate of Need list with end dates and a rental act from 2018.

Tennessee's rules, grouped by the body that writes them: TDEC first, then the Health Facilities Commission, the legislature's rental act, TABC and Human Services.

TDEC: decentralized sewer systems

A bill on failing wastewater systems, reported on April 7, 2026 as passed by the legislature, sets new rules for developer-built decentralized systems, gives TDEC until July 31, 2027 to write them, and lets their permits lapse after 12 months 25.

Health Facilities Commission: Certificate of Need, category by category

Certificate of Need still covers hospitals, nursing homes and several other facility types, but the list is shrinking on a fixed timetable. The Health Facilities Commission's current categories include hospitals, nursing homes, ambulatory surgical treatment centers, home care organizations, rehabilitation facilities and satellite emergency and inpatient facilities 14. On December 1, 2025, the requirement ended for intellectual disability habilitation facilities, burn units, NICUs, and MRI and PET services 14. Public Chapter 985 of 2024, with exemptions effective July 1, 2025, exempted hospital-run free-standing emergency departments within 10 miles of their main campus and at least 10 miles from another hospital or such department, and counties with no licensed acute care hospital, with listed exceptions such as nursing homes, home care and rehabilitation 15. A secondary consultancy reports that ambulatory surgical treatment centers leave the program on December 1, 2027, under new licensure requirements 16. SB 1369, enacted in the 2026 session, repeals the requirement for satellite emergency departments and cardiac catheterization on July 1, 2028, and for acute care hospitals on July 1, 2030, replacing it with licensure carrying TennCare, charity care and payor-mix conditions 17.

A memory care licensing category or unit threshold was not found. Our assisted living and memory care and medical office and ASC pages set out the demand method.

The Short-Term Rental Unit Act and the permits on top of it

The Short-Term Rental Unit Act, effective May 17, 2018, partly preempts local rules: it protects short-term rentals that predate a local ordinance, and that protection ends on sale or transfer, after 30 continuous months of non-use or after 3 violations 24. Local permits sit on top of the Act; the Sevier County section below sets out the county and Gatlinburg regimes. A state hotel occupancy tax and typical local hotel tax rates were not found, and neither was licensing for campgrounds and RV parks.

TABC: package stores, grocery wine, servers and hemp

Tennessee caps how many retail package store interests one owner may hold, with a lower cap in municipalities that allow few stores 20; a market in package store licence transfers, or a typical acquisition cost, was not found. Under the state alcohol code, municipalities may not limit the number of retail food store wine licences 21, which matters to any grocery or convenience store study counting on wine sales. Liquor server permits issued from January 1, 2025 run for two years, replacing the older longer term 22. TABC's permanent rules for hemp-derived cannabinoid products took effect on June 9, 2026, and a TABC licence is needed to sell them after June 30, 2026 23.

Human Services: child care in tiers

The Department of Human Services licenses child care in tiers by the number of children in care: family child care homes for 5 to 7 children, group child care homes for 8 to 12 and child care centers for 13 or more, with operations caring for 4 or fewer eligible for voluntary registration rather than licensing 18. Ratios are less settled: a secondary site publishes a ratio table by age group 19, other sites disagree, and no primary-source table was found.

Review Failures

A legacy rental sold, hotel rooms counted early, Memphis visitors and dated employer lists.

The 2018 rental act, and three market records whose dates a reviewer can check against the study's own.

  1. A legacy rental bought as if its protection came with it

    The Short-Term Rental Unit Act shields a rental that predates a local ordinance, and that shield ends on sale or transfer 24. A purchase case that carries the seller's legacy standing over to the buyer counts on protection the act ends at closing.

  2. A Chattanooga competitive set counted before the openings

    Hamilton County's count of about 11,500 hotel rooms dates from November 2025, the month the Times Free Press listed the openings still to come, The Waymark's 148 rooms among them 57.

  3. Memphis visitor demand taken as rising

    Memphis Tourism counted 10.3 million visitors in calendar 2025, below the 2024 total 47.

  4. Citing 2024 employer lists as current

    The Tri-Cities and Jackson employer figures on this page come from broker reports of January and February 2024 6168.

Site Tests

Tennessee site checks: the Certificate of Need list, the sewer record and TVA's data center rate.

The Certificate of Need list and TVA's rate class carry dates; the sewer check rests on TACIR's 2024 counts and the 2026 bill's dates.

A health care use is checked first against the Certificate of Need list as it will stand when the project files, because the list under rules loses categories on dates set through 2030 1417. Assisted care living facilities and residential homes for the aged are licensed rather than listed, so for them the check is the licence.

The sewer check depends on which system takes the flow. TACIR's moratoria and enforcement orders, counted at the top of this page, affect particular permittees and local governments 26, so the question is whether the subject's provider is one of them; Spring Hill's list of projects with vested rights, tied to its consent order with TDEC, is described on our Nashville page. A site planned on a developer-built decentralized system sits under the bill reported as passed in April 2026 instead: it gives TDEC until July 31, 2027 to write its rules and lets permits under it lapse after 12 months 25. The file therefore carries the permit's issue date next to the planned start of construction.

For a data center, the power check is a rate class. TVA's data center rate under power applies its Capacity Commitment Charge to new or expanding loads above 5 MW 34, so the site's planned load is set against that threshold before any cost is carried into the budget.

Memphis

Memphis and Shelby County: fewer residents than in 2020, in a housing market that crosses two state lines.

Shelby County's population stood below its 2020 base and the city's visitor count below the year before.

Shelby County held 910,226 residents on July 1, 2025, below its April 2020 estimates base of 930,012 44. It authorized 1,230 new private housing units in 2025 44. Nonfarm employment in the Memphis metro was 656.5 thousand in August 2025 and a preliminary 653.3 thousand in August 2026 45. Memphis drew 10.3 million visitors in calendar 2025, below the 2024 total, by Memphis Tourism's count 47.

HUD's 2024 market analysis for the Memphis area counted 1,950 rental units under construction 46. HUD defines that housing market area as Memphis, TN-MS-AR 46, so a Memphis trade area can reach into Mississippi and Arkansas.

Data center building is one more Memphis record. xAI filed a permit valued at $659.3M on March 3, 2026 for an expansion of its Colossus 2 site 42; DatacenterDynamics put the new building at 312,000 SF and reported Colossus 2 online in January 2026 43. An unattributed ArcGIS StoryMap records that temporary gas turbines were permitted for xAI in Shelby County on July 2, 2025, and that the Air Pollution Control Board dismissed an appeal of that permit, 6-1, on December 15, 2025 48.

Knoxville

Knoxville and Knox County: two pipeline counts.

Knox County is growing and building. Its apartment pipeline is counted by two brokers and its hotels by one tourism office.

Knox County reached 511,453 residents on July 1, 2025, up from an estimates base of 478,925 in April 2020 49, and authorized 5,013 new private housing units in 2025 49. Nonfarm employment in the Knoxville metro was 463.6 thousand in November 2025 50.

Matthews counted 2,980 multifamily units under construction in Knoxville in Q2 2026 51; MMG Real Estate Advisors counted 2,827 in the same quarter 52. The hotel market is measured by Visit Knoxville from STR data: Knox County hotels ran 65.8% occupancy, a $126.40 average daily rate and $83.14 RevPAR, selling 1,771,785 room nights, from January to September 2025 53. Visit Knoxville's annual report, using CoStar data, describes occupancy as flat for the fiscal year to June 2026 54.

Chattanooga

Chattanooga and Hamilton County: a dated employer base and a hotel wave.

Chattanooga is the one Tennessee market where the major employers have reported current headcounts. The pressure point is hotel supply.

Hamilton County held 390,833 residents on July 1, 2025, up from an estimates base of 366,840 56, and authorized 1,887 new private housing units in 2025 56. The Chattanooga Area Chamber's 2026 major employers list, with self-reported headcounts as of December 31, 2025, is the most current primary employer table the research found 55. It is led by BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee with 6,303 full-time employees, followed by Erlanger Health with 5,681 full-time and Hamilton County Schools with 5,605 full-time 55. Volkswagen Chattanooga reported 3,937 full-time, the Tennessee Valley Authority 3,748 full-time in the area, CHI Memorial 3,645 full-time and UT Chattanooga 1,536 full-time 55.

Hamilton County's hotel inventory was about 11,500 rooms in November 2025 57, and the Times Free Press listed a wave of new openings, among them The Waymark, a Tapestry Collection by Hilton hotel with 148 rooms, opening on December 29, 2025 57. The research found no ADR or RevPAR series for the county.

Pigeon Forge / Gatlinburg

Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg and Sevier County: a visitor market on a small resident base.

Sevier County's residents are a fraction of its demand; visitors and permitted short-term rentals set the market.

Sevier County held 101,342 residents on July 1, 2025, against an estimates base of 98,677 in April 2020, and authorized 1,139 new private housing units in 2025 58. Those figures understate the market, because demand for rooms, cabins, restaurants and attractions in Pigeon Forge and Gatlinburg comes from visitors to the Smoky Mountains rather than from residents. The research found no official hotel occupancy, ADR or RevPAR series for the county, only secondary market reports.

New supply is arriving in Pigeon Forge. Drury Plaza Pigeon Forge opened with 252 rooms in August 2025, and Staybridge Suites Pigeon Forge opened on March 20, 2026, by a secondary listing of new hotels 59. Short-term rental supply runs under permits: since January 1, 2024, unincorporated Sevier County, outside Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Sevierville and Pittman Center, has required an annual short-term rental unit permit, and Gatlinburg requires an annual Tourist Residency Permit 60, on top of the state Short-Term Rental Unit Act's legacy protections 24.

Two permit regimes, one for the unincorporated county and one for Gatlinburg, mean that a cabin's competitive set can sit under different rules on either side of a city line. Our Townsend cabin resort case, set in unincorporated Blount County outside Townsend, shows how a study measures a mountain rental market when the data vendors disagree.

Tri-Cities

The Tri-Cities, Sullivan and Washington counties: Johnson City, Kingsport and Bristol.

The Tri-Cities record holds county population, a January 2024 broker report and one credit action.

The research covers two counties. Sullivan County held 163,759 residents on July 1, 2025, against an estimates base of 158,146 63. Washington County held 141,199 against a base of 132,999 64.

The employer evidence is a January 2024 broker report. It credits Ballad Health with 13,800 employees across 29 counties and East Tennessee State University with an enrollment of 13,378 61. It also records a $23M Citigroup facility in Boones Creek, in northern Washington County, from April 2023 61. In December 2025, S&P revised its credit outlook on Ballad Health to positive, according to the Johnson City Press 62.

Clarksville

Clarksville and Montgomery County: Fort Campbell and a wave of industrial announcements.

A county still adding residents, and an industrial record.

Montgomery County held 249,935 residents on July 1, 2025, up from an estimates base of 220,012 in April 2020 66, and authorized 2,723 new private housing units in 2025 66. Fort Campbell anchors the market, and the county's economic development council reports a steady monthly flow of soldiers separating from the post 65.

The industrial story is large. On December 15, 2025 the county announced Korea Zinc's $6.6 billion project at the former Nyrstar site on Moore Road, described as the largest foreign direct investment announced in the state, with new and retained jobs and a payment-in-lieu-of-tax incentive 65.

Montgomery County levies an adequate facilities tax on new residential development for school facilities 67; the research holds no dated rate. Current headcounts for Fort Campbell, Austin Peay and the school system were not found.

Jackson

Madison County and Jackson: a regional hospital, large plants and the BlueOval City question.

West Tennessee Healthcare's flagship, large manufacturers and a university, in a county growing slowly, with BlueOval City outside it.

Worked Cases

Tennessee's worked cases: A-frame cabins outside Townsend, a new restaurant in Sevierville and a medical office in Lebanon.

Three Tennessee cases so far: one in unincorporated Blount County on the quieter side of the Smokies, one in Sevier County and one in Wilson County.

Blount County and the Smoky Mountains. The cabin resort outside Townsend tests whether a small resort of new A-frame cabins on acreage in unincorporated Blount County can carry SBA 504 debt. It works through park visitation that has softened while county visitor spending rose, two short-term rental data vendors that disagree on occupancy, and a large competing resort due to open nearby. It concludes that the market supports the product at upper-middle performance, and that the loan should be sized to median-plus RevPAR rather than to the top quartile, which leaves the sponsor carrying a much larger share of the cost than the structure it tests first.

Sevierville and Lebanon. The Sevierville restaurant case tests a new-build restaurant in the Sevier County tourism corridor against an SBA 7(a) loan, and the Lebanon medical office case tests a medical office building with an ambulatory surgery center in Wilson County, in the Nashville metro area, against an SBA 504 loan.

Where the other markets stand. No published case names a location in Memphis, Chattanooga, the Tri-Cities, Clarksville or Jackson, and none names a site in Nashville itself. The Townsend case does reach two other sections of this page by reference: it sets Pigeon Forge's rental market beside Townsend's as a comparison, and it cites passenger traffic at the Knoxville airport among its demand indicators.

Tennessee Questions

Tennessee feasibility study questions.

Where did Tennessee's population gain of the year to July 2025 come from?

Net domestic migration was the largest component. The state gained 63,785 residents, or 0.88% 2, to reach 7,315,076 on July 1, 2025 1. Births outran deaths by only 3,597, while net domestic migration added 42,389 and net international migration 17,990, in a Census-derived count republished by a secondary map tool 3.

How does that year compare with Tennessee's record 2024 gain?

It was smaller. The State Data Center revised the gain of the year to July 2024 to 98,262, the largest in the state's history 24; the year to July 2025 added 63,785 2.

How much new lodging and housing has Sevier County added lately?

In Pigeon Forge, Drury Plaza opened with 252 rooms in August 2025 and Staybridge Suites followed on March 20, 2026, by a secondary listing of new hotels 59. The county authorized 1,139 new private housing units in 2025, on a resident base of 101,342 at July 1, 2025 58. Nightly rentals are counted through permits: unincorporated Sevier County has required an annual short-term rental unit permit since January 1, 2024, and Gatlinburg issues an annual Tourist Residency Permit 60.

How does Tennessee's sales tax enter a feasibility study?

Through pricing and the project budget. Tennessee's average combined sales tax rate was 9.61% at midyear 2026, of which local governments added an average of 2.61% 27. How construction materials and business equipment are taxed was not found.

Does a health care project in Tennessee still need a Certificate of Need?

For the facility types still on the list, yes, and the list is shrinking on a timetable. Hospitals, nursing homes, ambulatory surgical treatment centers, home care organizations and rehabilitation facilities remain on it 14; MRI, PET, burn units, NICUs and habilitation facilities left it on December 1, 2025 14. Surgery centers move to licensure on December 1, 2027 16, and SB 1369 repeals the requirement for satellite emergency departments and cardiac catheterization on July 1, 2028 and for acute care hospitals on July 1, 2030 17.

What should a Tennessee feasibility study say about sewer capacity?

Whether the system that would take the flow is under a moratorium or a TDEC order. With data as of May 2024, TACIR's draft report on wastewater systems counted both kinds across the state 26, and the counts open this page. A site on a developer-built decentralized system answers to the April 2026 bill described under rules instead.

What did the five Tennessee counties on this page with 2025 permit counts authorize?

Knox County authorized 5,013 new private housing units in 2025 49, Montgomery County 2,723 66, Hamilton County 1,887 56, Shelby County 1,230 44 and Sevier County 1,139 58.
