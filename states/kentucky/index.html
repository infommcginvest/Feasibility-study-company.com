For assisted living, the 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate counts 784,676 Kentuckians aged 65 and over 21. The statute separates the three licences by what each may offer: the community licence provides no basic health services, the basic health care licence runs no secured unit, and a secured dementia unit may not operate without the dementia care licence 29. An older federal compendium, from 2007, defines an assisted living community as a series of living units for five or more unrelated adults, with units of at least 200 square feet subject to exceptions; the research marks that definition to be checked against the current statute 91. For child care, Type I staff ratios are set by age band: 1:5 for infants with a maximum group of 10, 1:6 for toddlers with 12, 1:10 at two to three years with 20, 1:12 at three to four with 24, 1:14 at four to five with 28 and 1:15 at five to seven with 30; for older school-age children the ratio is 1:20 over a full day, in groups of up to 30, and 1:25 before and after school 30.

Kentucky's Certificate of Need statute reaches health facilities, services and major medical equipment, and a capital expenditure above an approved certificate needs an administrative escalation or a new certificate; the expenditure minimums are adjusted each year 8. The Certificate of Need repeals Tennessee has scheduled are set out on our Tennessee page.

The tank branch still publishes claims agendas, and on July 9, 2025 it issued a notice on reimbursing water used during a tank closure 15.

Beside the $7.25 minimum in the table, Kentucky's tipped minimum wage is $2.13 an hour 33.

How many net international migrants did Jefferson County gain in the year to July 2025?

Warren County, home to Bowling Green, by the Kentucky Association of Counties' April 2026 reading of the Census county file: 14,336 people, 2,809 of them in the year to July 2025 19.

How many residential units did Fayette County, home to Lexington, permit in 2025?

Who runs air cargo at the Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky airport?

DHL Express runs an operation of 194 acres there, as The Lane Report described it in June 2025 50, and Amazon Air's hub is in Hebron's cargo district 51.

Where are new apartments being built around Bowling Green, Kentucky?

Ewing Point at Mount Victor is listed on the corridor of I-65 Exit 26 and Lovers Lane 59, and Grove Park, listed as built in 2025, on Nashville Road (US 31W) 60. In January 2025 the Kirkland Company counted 1 property of 255 units under construction 58.

Did Kentucky pass Certificate of Need reform in 2025 or 2026?

Neither year's did. HB 354, HB 355 and HB 609 went to House Health Services in February 2025 and none advanced 9. In 2026 HB 407 got through that committee by 10 votes to 5, then failed before the session adjourned in April 10.

How has Pike County's population changed since 2020?

It fell to 54,721 by July 1, 2025, a drop of 6.7% from 58,667 at the April 2020 base, in Census QuickFacts at Vintage 2025, and 14 housing units were permitted there in 2025 18.
