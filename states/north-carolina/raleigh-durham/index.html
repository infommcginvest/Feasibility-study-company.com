Growth

One region, three census geographies.

The federal tables describe the Triangle three ways, and each answers a different question. The Raleigh-Durham-Cary combined statistical area held 2,484,238 people in 2025, up from 2,242,332 in 2020 1, and its total takes in counties outside the seven this page covers. Within it, the Raleigh-Cary metropolitan area of Wake, Johnston and Franklin counties reached 1,595,720 residents in 2025 2. The Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area of Chatham, Durham, Orange and Person counties stood at 625,485 on July 1, 2025, against 618,157 a year before 3. The combined area's 2020 and 2025 figures are both as City Population tabulates them; the Durham-Chapel Hill pair are two July estimates a year apart. Those seven counties are the ones this page covers.

Across the seven counties, the 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate counts 822,551 households, with 285,635 renter households among them 16; this page does not split either count between the two metros.

Economic Base

Raleigh-Cary hiring, a plant announced in Benson and a grant cancelled in Durham.

Raleigh-Cary payrolls reached 788,600 in June 2026, up 20,300 from the 768,300 of June 2025 4. The series is the Raleigh-Cary metro: Wake, Johnston and Franklin counties. Income reaches all seven counties: per capita personal income, a mean over residents, was $78,076 in calendar 2024, and total personal income was $170,402.9 million in calendar 2024 14.

Two 2026 project stories sit in the record. Vulcan Elements' rare-earth magnet plant in Benson, Johnston County, was reported with $918.1 million of announced investment and 1,000 jobs, and the report does not say whether construction has begun or when hiring starts 5. In Durham County, Carolina Journal reported that the state's Economic Investment Committee terminated the JDIG award for a $125 million project of Jaguar Gene Therapy and Advance Medicine Partners on February 24, 2026 6. Each is cited here at the stage its source reports.

Districts

Downtown Durham, North Hills, Hub RTP and Brier Creek: the places the record names.

The Triangle's commercial record locates demand by named district. TenantBase names downtown Durham, North Hills in Raleigh and Hub RTP as the areas where tenant activity gathered in Q1 2026 12, and the Wake County tourism report, as ABC11 relays it, points to Brier Creek for hotel traffic and restaurant growth 7. Brier Creek is the one place the record ties to hotels, so a hotel there can cite it directly; a hotel, restaurant or office site elsewhere in the seven counties finds no named district in this record.

The Greensboro, Winston-Salem and High Point, Asheville and Fayetteville records are set out on the state page, and our Nashville page will cover that Tennessee market.

Supply by Asset Class

Yardi's March occupancy, Wake County's hotel year and Colliers' retail quarter.

Multifamily: Yardi's Raleigh market

Yardi Matrix put stabilized occupancy in its Raleigh multifamily market at 93.1% in March 2026, down 70 basis points, and counted an average asking rent of $1,539 in April 2026 and 2,157 units delivered between January and April 2026 10. The occupancy reading is for March and the deliveries cover January to April; Yardi reports both, and neither figure says how many of the new units have leased. The research found no count of apartments under construction for the Triangle, though our agency multifamily case cites a Yardi Matrix count of units underway in Raleigh-Durham.

Hotels: Wake County's year

In 2025, Wake County's hotel occupancy was 67.7% and its average daily rate $137.55 8. Visit Raleigh reports 2025 changes of minus 2.9% for occupancy and plus 1.4% for the average daily rate, without stating the comparison base 8. RevPAR for January to September 2025 was $93.10 9; the full-year figure was not found. County Business Patterns counted 8,935 employees in hotels (except casino hotels) and motels across the seven counties in 2023 15, the one lodging measure on this page that reaches Durham, Orange, Johnston and the outer counties. The hotel year on this page is Wake's alone, from Wake's tourism office; for a hotel feasibility study in any of the other six counties, the research gathered no county hotel series.

Retail: Colliers' Q2 2026 quarter

Colliers reports retail asking rent of $24.80 per sq ft NNN for Raleigh-Durham in Q2 2026, with 537,600 sq ft under construction and vacancy below 3% in the same quarter 11. For retail sites, Colliers gives these three readings for its Raleigh-Durham market rather than for any one county.

Senior housing: a partial establishment count

In the 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate, 297,926 residents aged 65 and over lived in the seven counties 16. County Business Patterns counted 116 establishments in 2023 in the County Business Patterns category for assisted living facilities for the elderly 15. That category only partly matches the class, so the count frames an assisted living market without listing its competitors. Beyond that establishment count, the research found no Triangle senior housing supply series.

Local Rules

Wake County's occupancy tax is the one local levy on record.

Wake County collected $41,460,586 in occupancy tax in 2025 8, a sum the tourism report publishes without the rate behind it, so it measures collections rather than the levy a new room will carry. It is the one county levy in the Triangle record; Durham, Orange and Johnston appear in it with no occupancy tax figure at all. The collections figure is for calendar 2025 and comes from the same Visit Raleigh report as the county's hotel year, so the two share a period.

The state page's site tests take up the sewer plant behind a parcel's line and the state licences that come ahead of a town's approval.

Hazards

Chantal put Durham and Orange counties on the disaster list in 2025.

After Tropical Depression Chantal, the SBA declared an administrative disaster for North Carolina on July 25, 2025, naming Durham and Orange counties as the primary counties 13; the Federal Register carried the notice on July 30, 2025. Physical damage loan applications were due by September 23, 2025, and economic injury applications by April 27, 2026 13. Both counties named sit in the Durham-Chapel Hill metro, inland rather than on the coast, so a lender underwriting a new project there has reason to ask whether the site, its access road or its utility lines were affected. The hazards section of our state page sets this declaration beside the other federal declarations it records.

Lenders and Capital

Seven counties' SBA credit, counted where the project is built.

For projects in the seven counties, the SBA's loan-level FOIA files show $1,612.1 million of 7(a) credit and $160.7 million of 504 debentures, each approved in fiscal years 2016 to 2025, net of cancellations 17. SBA's power to require a study sits in 13 CFR 120.160(b): SBA may require professional appraisals, a survey, or a feasibility study 18, and for debt service coverage its SOP gives the standard 7(a) figure as 1.15x, with the basis given as historical and/or projected, with 1:1 global coverage; start-ups reach 1.15x within two years 19.

Triangle Questions

The Triangle questions this page's census, broker and tourism figures answer.

Raleigh-Cary, Durham-Chapel Hill or the combined statistical area: which count fits a Triangle site?

The one whose geography holds the subject's county. A Wake, Johnston or Franklin County site starts from Raleigh-Cary, 1,595,720 in 2025 2; a Chatham, Durham, Orange or Person County site from Durham-Chapel Hill, 625,485 on July 1, 2025 3. The Raleigh-Durham-Cary combined statistical area counted 2,484,238 people in 2025 1, a total that also takes in counties outside this page's seven. The two metro counts reach this page through different publishers, City Population and FRED, so the text names which one it uses.

Were Raleigh apartments still losing occupancy in 2026?

Yes, in Yardi Matrix's March reading: stabilized occupancy of 93.1%, down 70 basis points as Yardi reports it 10. The same report counts 2,157 units delivered from January to April 2026 and an April asking rent of $1,539 10.

How did Wake County hotels do in 2025?

Wake hotels ran 67.7% occupancy at a $137.55 average daily rate in 2025 8, and RevPAR for January to September 2025 was $93.10 9. Each of these is a Wake County figure, published by the county's tourism office.

Can a Johnston County project count on the Benson magnet plant?

Not on the announcement alone. Expansion Solutions Magazine reported Vulcan Elements' rare-earth magnet plant in Benson, Johnston County, with $918.1 million of announced investment, and the report states no construction status 5. On this page the plant is an announcement with a dollar figure and a job count, and no construction date.

Where does the Triangle record place office and hotel demand?

In four named places, from two sources. TenantBase names downtown Durham, North Hills in Raleigh and Hub RTP as the areas where tenant activity gathered in Q1 2026 12, and the Wake County tourism report, as ABC11 relays it, notes hotel traffic and restaurant growth at Brier Creek 7.

What did the SBA's Chantal declaration cover, and are its deadlines still open?

The SBA declared an administrative disaster on July 25, 2025 after Tropical Depression Chantal, naming Durham and Orange as the primary counties 13. Physical damage applications closed on September 23, 2025 and economic injury applications on April 27, 2026 13. Both deadlines have passed.

Is Triangle retail space still being built?

Yes. In Q2 2026 Colliers had 537,600 sq ft of Raleigh-Durham retail under construction, with asking rent at $24.80 per sq ft NNN 11. Both are Colliers' figures for its Raleigh-Durham market as a whole.

This page carries a hotel year for Wake County alone. A study elsewhere in the Triangle builds its own.

A hotel in Wake starts from Visit Raleigh's 2025 figures and one in any of the other six counties from its own competitive set; an apartment project from Yardi Matrix's Raleigh readings; a retail center from Colliers' Q2 2026 quarter. Each comes with its date, and the program and the loan request decide which of them the study leans on.
