The New York Thesis

New York's 2026 file starts at the grid: deficits forecast downstate, chip plants rising upstate.

In October 2025 NYISO, which runs New York's grid, published a short-term reliability assessment that placed the grid's first forecast deficits in New York City and on Long Island, starting in summer 2026 7. A project in either market that needs new or larger electric service carries that forecast into its budget.

The new money is elsewhere. The Governor's office counts more than $125 billion of semiconductor investment announced upstate under the current administration 3, with sites at Micron's fab campus in the Town of Clay, outside Syracuse, and GlobalFoundries' at Malta, in Saratoga County. Their schedules are dated in the Syracuse and Capital Region sections, and neither site sits in the two markets the grid forecast singles out.

Between the grid and the market stand New York's own statutes. A private project costing more than $5 million pays prevailing wage once public money covers 30% of its construction cost 8. Hospitals, nursing homes, surgery centers, home health agencies and hospices go through Certificate of Need 9.

  • SBA district offices Metro New York, Buffalo, Syracuse

NYISO published the margin with its parts. Hodgson Russ's summary of the April release sets 34,615 MW of resources against a peak forecast of 31,578 MW and required reserves of 2,620 MW, which leaves the 417 MW baseline, and gives a shortfall of 1,679 MW for an extended 95 degree heatwave 1.

The queue is the operator's other number. The Regional Plan Association, reading NYISO's Power Trends 2026, counts 51 large-load projects seeking 12,670 MW by May 2026, where 2022 had six projects and 1,045 MW, and reports that of the more than 100 generation projects approved since 2019, 14 were under construction 2. NYISO's 2026 Gold Book carries a separate tally in its Table IV-7, load interconnection requests of 12,330.1 MW for summer and 12,196.1 MW for winter as of March 15, 2026 10. The two come from different documents and dates; a study quotes one, with its date, and never adds them.

Where the October 2025 assessment placed the deficits matters for the rest of this page. New York City and Long Island are also the two markets inside the downstate minimum wage zone 11, so a project there that needs new or larger service carries both the labor floor and the first forecast shortfall.

New York City

New York City: rooms still opening, and apartments taxed at a multiple of homes.

The Bronx, Brooklyn (Kings County), Manhattan (New York County), Queens and Staten Island (Richmond County).

The State Comptroller's October 2026 report on the city's hotels counts 124,000 active rooms in an average month of 2025 and 45,325 hotel jobs, a job count still 12.9% under 2019 4. Citing CoStar, it lists 24 projects with 5,778 rooms in active development through 2028, and 4,852 rooms expected to open in 2026, more than in any other U.S. market 4. Building has moved outward: from 2020 to 2025 the hotel count rose 62.5% in the Bronx, 25.6% in Brooklyn and 20.1% in Queens 4. CoStar's own January 2026 release ranked the city first among U.S. markets for 2025 on occupancy, average rate and RevPAR, at 84.1%, $333.71 and $280.71 16. About 16,000 rooms have served as shelter capacity 4, and a hotel study places each of those properties in or out of its competitive set by name.

The Rent Guidelines Board's 2026 Housing Supply Report, drawing on the 2023 Housing and Vacancy Survey, the latest published, gives a citywide net rental vacancy of 1.41%, running from 0.82% in the Bronx to 2.33% in Manhattan, with 33,210 vacant units available for rent 5. Taxes then weigh on the same buildings: the NYU Furman Center's reading of the Lincoln Institute's study for taxes paid in 2025 puts the city's apartment tax rate at 5.67 times its homestead rate, the widest gap in the study, and the city's apartment rate fifth highest among its peers 17. The last commercial rate in hand is older, 3.96% on a $1 million property for taxes paid in 2015 18. A multifamily study here states the building's tax class before it states a rent.

Census QuickFacts counts 8,584,629 New York City residents on July 1, 2025, where the April 2020 census had 8,804,190, and gives the decline measured from the 2020 estimates base as 2.5% 19. The Department of Labor's September 2026 briefing counts 4,894,900 nonfarm jobs in the city region, 79,200 more than a year before 20. BLS draws the wider New York-Newark-Jersey City metro across New York, New Jersey and Pennsylvania, and counted 10,176,200 jobs there in June 2026, 69,100 more than in June 2025, a gain BLS judged not statistically significant 21.

Three inputs a city study has to build for itself: an annual five-borough permit count, a ranked employer list with headcounts, and any supply figure for storage, industrial space, retail or senior housing. The Comptroller credits the hotel industry with $2.4 billion of city tax revenue in fiscal 2025 4; the hotel tax rate itself was not found, so the room tax in a pro forma comes from the city's current schedule.

Long Island

Long Island: Nassau and Suffolk, and a health system cutting positions.

Two suburban counties east of the city, in SBA's Metro New York District and the downstate wage zone.

Northwell Health, a system with more than 100,000 workers and about a dozen Long Island hospitals, confirmed on October 5, 2026 that it was eliminating positions again, without giving a number, News 12 reported 22. A study that counts on hospital staff or patient visits states that the cuts were under way at its date, of a size not disclosed. The Department of Labor's July 2026 briefing counts 1,400,200 nonfarm jobs in the region, up 4,100 over the year, with the private sector up 4,500 23.

Nassau County permitted 821 housing units in 2025 and 830 in 2024 24; Suffolk County permitted 1,399 and 1,407 25. Hudson Valley / Mid-Hudson

The Hudson Valley: Manhattan's SBA district, the upstate wage.

Orange and Dutchess counties form the Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh metro; Ulster County is the Kingston metro and Sullivan County the Monticello micropolitan area.

Two rules draw different lines through this market. SBA serves Dutchess, Orange, Sullivan and Ulster counties from its Metro New York District, the same district as the five boroughs, Long Island, Putnam, Rockland and Westchester 26. The minimum wage does not follow the district: the four counties pay the $16.00 rate set for the rest of the state, while Westchester pays the downstate $17.00 11. A labor-heavy business here budgets the upstate floor, whichever district office serves it.

Orange County permitted 1,668 housing units in 2025 against 2,172 in 2024 27, and Dutchess County permitted 552 in 2025 28. Whether the drop means a tighter market or a pipeline already delivered is something only a count of recent openings in the trade area can show, because no vacancy, rent, occupancy or supply figure was found in any of the four counties. Nor was an employer list, an anchor institution, a large announced project or a payroll series.

Albany / Capital Region

The Capital Region: state government in Albany, chip fabs at Malta.

The Albany-Schenectady-Troy metro, from Albany County north into Saratoga County.

Two economies share the metro. Albany is the state capital, and the Capital Region Chamber's 2025 economic profile names its large private employers, from Albany Med Health System, St. Peter's and CDPHP to GE Aerospace and GE Vernova, Plug Power, Hannaford and GlobalFoundries, without headcounts; the same profile counts 10,122 semiconductor cluster jobs 29.

The chip record is dated and large. In February 2024 the state announced a ten-year Fab 8 expansion at Malta of $11.6 billion, with a new 358,000 square foot fab 30. In January 2025 GlobalFoundries announced an advanced packaging and photonics center there, $575 million including $186 million of research and development over ten years 31, and in June 2025 it raised its plan for its New York and Vermont fabs to $16 billion 32. The last figure spans two states, so none of it is credited to Saratoga County without the company's own split.

Albany County permitted 683 housing units in 2025 and 741 in 2024 33. Saratoga County's permits, a payroll change for the metro and any supply count for the region were not found. A workforce housing or extended-stay study near Malta ties its demand to a fab milestone the company or the state has dated, and shows the loan surviving a later date.

Syracuse / Central NY

Syracuse and Onondaga County, where the fab site in Clay sets the calendar.

Onondaga County, with the city of Syracuse and the Town of Clay, at the center of a metro that also takes in Madison and Oswego counties.

Micron's own words date the project. The company broke ground on January 16, 2026 and called the site the largest private investment in the state's history 34; the state reported the first foundations poured by July 9 3. The Governor's groundbreaking release set the long-run expectation at 9,000 jobs on the site and as many as 50,000 across the region within 30 years 35. A study that draws on those numbers uses only the milestones already reached and shows coverage holding if the next ones come later.

Onondaga County permitted 1,701 housing units in 2025 36. The Department of Labor's May 2026 job trends report counted 2,800 more jobs in the Syracuse area over the year to March 2026 37, and QuickFacts puts the city of Syracuse at 144,896 residents on July 1, 2025, 2.5% below its 2020 estimates base 38. An apartment study in Onondaga County counts the 2025 permits that deliver during its own lease-up years.

Sewer and water work for the plant is reported but not yet verified. CNY Signal, a local outlet, in an undated report not checked against county records, describes a bond authorization by the county Legislature for an upgrade of the Oak Orchard wastewater plant and a water authority contract for a new main to the fab site 39; a lender checks both against the Legislature's and the authority's own records.

Syracuse holds SBA's district office for much of upstate New York and USDA Rural Development's state office 4041. M&T Bank's November 2025 release ranks it first among 7(a) lenders in the Syracuse District for fiscal 2025, with 160 loans and $11.7 million; its release of a year earlier gave 113 loans and $13.1 million for the district 4243.

Buffalo

Buffalo and Erie County: a city tax on every room night since September 2025, and a vacant downtown hotel.

The Buffalo-Cheektowaga metro, centered on Erie County; the hotel and permit figures are the county's.

Health care leads the employer list. Invest Buffalo Niagara's list, drawn from the 2026 Buffalo Business First Book of Lists, puts Kaleida Health first with 9,553 workers, followed by Catholic Health, and counts 4,497 at Tops Markets and 3,945 at Roswell Park Comprehensive Cancer Center 45. Metro payrolls were 566,900 in June 2025 and 567,400 in June 2026, a change BLS called not statistically significant 21. QuickFacts counts 274,613 residents in the city on July 1, 2025, 1.3% under its 2020 base 46, and Erie County permitted 1,213 housing units in 2025 and 1,208 in 2024 47.

The county's hotel record comes from its visitors bureau. Visit Buffalo Niagara's November 2025 report gives Erie County hotels 63.5% occupancy, a $145.14 average rate and $92.20 RevPAR for 2025 through November, on room revenue of $311,891,061; November alone ran 60.1%, $149.53 and $89.91 48. Short-term rentals in the county ran 34.6% occupancy, a $233 rate and $81 RevPAR over the same months 48. The bureau's first-quarter 2026 report cites a study finding that 400 additional rooms downtown would leave average occupancy in the middle 60s 49.

Three city decisions of 2025 reach a hotel pro forma. Since September 1, 2025 the City of Buffalo has charged a 3% occupancy tax on the room rate of hotels, motels, bed and breakfasts and short-term rentals, with a $1,500 penalty for each occupancy and day it is not charged 50; before that levy, Visit Buffalo Niagara put the combined bed tax at 13.75%, WIVB reported 51. The Common Council approved the $622 million city budget that carried the tax, 6 to 2, with an 8% property tax increase from July 1, 2025 52. And on June 19, 2025 the city began Article 19 proceedings to take over the vacant 489-room Buffalo Grand Hotel for eventual sale 53. A downtown hotel study runs one case with that property reopened and charges the city tax on top of the bed tax in each.

M&T Bank ranks itself first among 7(a) lenders in SBA's Buffalo District for fiscal 2025, with 285 loans and $39.4 million; its fiscal 2024 release gave 186 loans and $18.9 million for Buffalo and Rochester 4243.

Costs and Insurance

When public money reaches 30% of construction cost, the wage scale changes.

Prevailing wage on subsidized private work, the wage floor by region, the property tax line and the insurer of last resort.

Public money and prevailing wage

Section 224-a of the Labor Law, as the Department of Labor's July 2026 overview of its Bureau of Public Work describes it, makes a private construction project of more than $5 million a covered project, paying prevailing wage, once public funds reach 30% of its total construction cost; the Public Subsidy Board decides coverage 8. Phillips Lytle, a law firm, reads local industrial development agency benefits as public funds for that test 65, so an IDA abatement that lifts the pro forma can also lift the labor budget.

Assembly Bill A6708 would cut the threshold to 20%, abolish the Public Subsidy Board and leave the decision to the Department of Labor; it was in the Assembly Labor Committee 6667. The board's own record has been narrow. A board member who leads the Building and Construction Trades Council told Construction Dive in March 2025 that over four years it had found six of 36 projects eligible 67. A study that assembles IDA benefits, state grants or tax-exempt bonds shows the budget under the current test and under the proposed one.

The wage floor

The minimum wage rose on January 1, 2026 to $17.00 an hour in New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester and $16.00 in the rest of the state, and from 2027 it is indexed to the CPI-W for the Northeast, Spectrum News reported in December 2025 11. Fox Rothschild, a law firm, notes that the Department of Labor had to post the 2027 rate by October 1, 2026 68; no posted rate was found, so a labor-heavy pro forma raises its 2027 wage line by the index rather than holding it flat.

Property tax

No statewide effective commercial rate is given here. The dated changes on this page are local: the City of Buffalo's 8% increase from July 2025 52 (see Buffalo) and New York City's apartment rate at a multiple of its homestead rate (see New York City). A New York study builds the tax line from the assessor's roll and any abatement agreement the project holds.

The FAIR Plan

The state's FAIR Plan, the New York Property Insurance Underwriting Association, is described with its residential and commercial policy counts and its exposure in the sponsor memo for Senate Bill S8583, a document dated only by its 2025 to 2026 session 6. A 2013 examination by the Department of Financial Services recorded a cap of $1.5 million on insurable real property under the plan 69. No approved commercial rate change or carrier withdrawal turned up for 2025 or 2026.

Rules by Asset Class

Health facilities, liquor and rentals under New York's own statutes.

The county rental registries of 2025, the 500-foot rule, Certificate of Need with its 2025 thresholds, and the assisted living tiers.

Short-term rentals

Article 12-D of the Real Property Law enables local rental rules rather than preempting them. Signed on December 21, 2024 and amended by Chapter 99 of the Laws of 2025 on February 28, 2025, it put registration in county registries that a county may opt out of by local law, requires platforms to report quarterly and brings short-term rentals into the state sales tax base 71. Avalara put the effective date at April 21, 2025 72. A lodging, glamping or campground study counts rental competitors from the county registry where the county keeps one.

Liquor

New York sets no statewide cap on liquor licenses, and the State Liquor Authority is the only licensing body, according to liquorlicenselibrary.org 73. The Authority's removal petition, dated March 2, 2026, carries the 500-foot rule: in a municipality of 20,000 people or more, a new on-premises license within 500 feet of three or more existing on-premises licensees requires consultation with the municipality or community board and a public hearing; the same form sets a $192 fee to move a license to new premises 74. A restaurant or hotel bar on a dense block maps the licensees around it, since a hearing moves the opening date.

Certificate of Need

Under Article 28 of the Public Health Law, Certificate of Need covers ambulatory surgery centers, diagnostic and treatment centers, hospitals, midwifery birth centers and nursing homes; Article 36 adds certified home health agencies and long-term home health care programs, and Article 40 hospices 9. A change of ownership, a new service, major medical equipment, renovation, construction or establishment can each open a review 9. The Public Health and Health Planning Council decides establishment and ownership changes and advises on major construction 75.

Amendments to 10 NYCRR Part 710, effective August 6, 2025, raised the thresholds for full review, widened the exemptions and opened a self-certification route, in Sheppard Mullin's account 76. The Council's December 4, 2025 agenda still carried a nursing home CON ad hoc committee and a discussion of procedures shifting to ambulatory surgery centers 77. A surgery center or medical office study names the review tier that applies after August 2025, because the tier sets the approval calendar.

Assisted living

The Department of Health licenses assisted living as an adult care facility for adults unrelated to the operator 78, in three forms: the Assisted Living Residence, the Enhanced residence that lets residents age in place with nursing support, and the Special Needs residence for dementia, which requires a special needs plan 79. Enhanced and Special Needs status is a certification on top of the license and may cover all of the licensed beds or only part of them 80. A memory care or assisted living study prices each bed at the certification it will actually hold.

New York Questions

New York feasibility study questions.

When does a private New York project with IDA benefits or state grants have to pay prevailing wage?

Once two tests are met: a total construction cost above $5 million and public funds at 30% or more of it, which makes the job a covered project under Labor Law section 224-a, with coverage decided by the Public Subsidy Board 8. IDA benefits count toward the public share in Phillips Lytle's reading 65, so an incentive package can carry a project across the line. Assembly Bill A6708 would lower the share to 20% and hand the decision to the Department of Labor 6667; in March 2025 a board member told Construction Dive that the board had found six of 36 projects eligible over four years 67. A study prices the labor budget twice, once under each test.

Can New York's FAIR Plan insure a commercial building, and up to what amount?

It can. The sponsor memo for Senate Bill S8583 gives the New York Property Insurance Underwriting Association's commercial and residential policy counts and its exposure 6. The cap on insurable real property stood at $1.5 million under the plan of operation when the Department of Financial Services examined the Association in 2013 69, and no later figure is in hand. The Association was also an addressee of the Department's circular letter of May 20, 2026 on disaster preparation and response 70.

What changed in New York's Certificate of Need rules on August 6, 2025 for a surgery center or nursing home?

The review tiers moved; the list of covered facilities did not. Sheppard Mullin's summary of the amendments to 10 NYCRR Part 710 records higher thresholds for full review, wider exemptions and a new self-certification route from that date 76. Nursing homes, hospitals and surgery centers stay on the Article 28 list, with the other facility types the Department of Health names 9, so a new center or home still files; what changed is the tier its cost and scope land in. Nursing home policy stayed in motion afterward: the agenda of the Public Health and Health Planning Council for December 4, 2025 listed an ad hoc committee on nursing home CON 77. A study for either facility states its tier and the approval calendar that follows from it.

What does Buffalo's 2025 hotel occupancy tax add to a room night, and who pays it?

A 3% charge on the room rate, collected by the City of Buffalo on stays from September 1, 2025; short-term rentals and bed and breakfasts owe it as hotels and motels do, and an operator that fails to charge it faces a $1,500 penalty per occupancy per day 50. The guest pays it on top of the combined bed tax that Visit Buffalo Niagara had put at 13.75% before the city levy, as WIVB reported in March 2025 51. A Buffalo lodging pro forma shows the guest's full tax line under the new levy.

What is New York City's rental vacancy rate, and what does it mean for a new apartment building?

1.41% citywide net rental vacancy, from the city's 2023 Housing and Vacancy Survey, the most recent published, with the Bronx at 0.82%, Manhattan at 2.33% and 33,210 vacant units available for rent, in the Rent Guidelines Board's May 2026 supply report 5. A lender reads that count beside the tax line: citing the Lincoln Institute's figures for taxes paid in 2025, the NYU Furman Center puts New York City apartments at 5.67 times the homestead rate 17, and the last commercial reading in hand, for taxes paid in 2015, is 3.96% on a $1 million property 18. A multifamily study states the building's borough and tax class and runs its rent against the tax bill those two imply.

Where are short-term rentals registered in New York, and how does a lodging study use the registry?

In the counties. Real Property Law Article 12-D, a December 21, 2024 statute that Chapter 99 of the Laws of 2025 amended on February 28, 2025, places short-term rental registration in county registries, lets a county opt out by local law, has platforms report quarterly and makes the rentals subject to state sales tax 71; Avalara dates the law's effect to April 21, 2025 72. Where a county keeps the registry, a hotel, glamping or campground study takes its rental competitors from it by count rather than by estimate. The one rental performance series on this page is Erie County's, where short-term rentals posted 34.6% occupancy with a $233 rate and $81 RevPAR for 2025 through November 48.

What is New York's federal major disaster declaration record?

DR-4839, declared on October 21, 2024 for severe storms and flooding, appears on FEMA's New York page beside a November 2024 fire management grant for the Jennings Creek Fire, FM-5547-NY 89. SBA declared an administrative disaster on August 20, 2025 for the June 2025 storms, declaring Madison County and including six contiguous counties 44, and the February 22 to 23, 2026 blizzard, which flooded parts of the coast, drew no major declaration 91. A study on a site in those counties or on that coast names the event and the federal record that goes with it.
