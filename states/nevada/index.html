Nevada in 2025 and 2026

Payrolls still rising, while a grid, a river and an insurance statute decide what gets built.

Nevada employers kept hiring into the summer of 2026: DETR counted 1,619,400 seasonally adjusted nonfarm jobs in August, and its unemployment rate of 4.8%, down from 5.2% a year before, was the first reading under 5% since February 2020 4. The changes that reach a pro forma in this period came from the supply side of a project instead. NV Energy's 2026 resource plan answers a queue of data-center requests with new gas, solar and battery supply 21. In southern Nevada the water authority's ban on watering non-functional turf with Colorado River water takes effect at the end of 2026, after a court order in January paused its turf designations 1213. And since the first day of 2026, a property policy written in Nevada may leave wildfire out 3.

The markets below follow from that. Las Vegas, read on all of Clark County, is written on its own page; Reno, Lake Tahoe, Carson City, Fernley, Elko, Winnemucca and Laughlin are sections of this one, each read on the counties its records name.

Power and Water

NV Energy's 2026 resource plan, the data-center requests in it, and the Tahoe supply the utility will redirect.

NV Energy filed its 2026 Integrated Resource Plan with the Public Utilities Commission of Nevada in May 2026. Volume 6 reports 16,530 MW of requests from bundled-service, high-load-factor data centers 2, and RCG Economics' count of formal requests, about 16,930 MW from 39 prospective customers, does not match it, so a study states which of the two it relies on 1. Reading the plan on July 21, 2026, RCG called the roughly 22,000 MW of data-center interest nearly three times the two utilities' combined 2025 system peak of nearly 8,250 MW 1. Signed large-load agreements come to about 6 GW, about 84% of them in northern Nevada 1.

The plan's answer is new supply. It proposes 1,223 MW of new gas capacity at the Fort Churchill station near Yerington, and, in RCG's reading, its Obsidian Plan seeks approval of 23 power purchase agreements covering 4,370 MW of solar and 5,405 MW of battery storage 1. RCG puts data centers at 5% of the utilities' sales today and 64% by 2046 on the plan's projection 1. In August 2026 the commission denied the Bureau of Consumer Protection's request for a public consumer session on the plan 14.

Part of the capacity is power Nevada now sends across the state line. In RCG's account of the notice NV Energy gave Liberty Utilities, about 75% of the supply for roughly 49,000 residents on Lake Tahoe's California shore ends by May 2027, and the utility means to serve data-center load in the Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center's area with it 1. Those are California customers, so no figure of theirs belongs to the Nevada shore in the Lake Tahoe section.

The state also offers data centers a tax abatement. GOED's terms, updated in August 2026, cut the sales and use tax rate to 2% for 10 or 20 years and abates 75% of personal property tax; the 20-year tier asks for 50 Nevada-resident full-time employees, wages at 100% of the statewide average, and $100 million of capital spending within five years, and at least 50% of the construction workers must be Nevada residents 10. Edison Electric Institute's September 2026 list carries Vantage Data Centers' announced Storey County campus at $3B and 224 MW 15. Reno, Nye County and Humboldt County adopted data-center moratoria during 2026, while Sparks and Clark County were weighing code changes 16.

Water in the Southern Nevada Water Authority's area

About 90% of the authority's supply is Colorado River water, by its fiscal 2022 federal grant application 12. The AB 356 deadline for non-functional turf at the end of 2026, and the court order of January 2026 that paused the authority's designations, are set out in the Las Vegas page's water section 1213. No state water-use rule for car washes was found, and no sewer moratorium; for a site in the authority's area, the parcel's turf designation is the water record a study reads.

Property Cover

A 2026 law that lets carriers exclude wildfire, and the homeowner cancellations counted before it.

AB 376, signed in June 2025, took effect on January 1, 2026. Since then an insurer may exclude wildfire from a property policy in Nevada or offer it as standalone cover, and either form needs the Division of Insurance to approve its policy form and its rate filing 3. There is no FAIR Plan or other insurer of last resort in Nevada, by a July 2026 trade report drawing on two Nevada newsrooms 6.

The homeowner record behind the law is older. The Division of Insurance's 2025 Market Report, as Nevada Current read it in October 2025, counted about 158,000 home cancellations and non-renewals in 2023, of which 481 were tied to wildfire, 82% more wildfire non-renewals than the year before 17. For 2024, The Nevada Independent put Washoe County at more than 1,600 cancellations or non-renewals of every kind, over 60% of the state total, with fewer than 1,100 across the other sixteen counties combined 18.

All of these are homeowners lines. No commercial property rate change approved in 2025 or 2026 and no exit of a commercial carrier was found, so a commercial file in Nevada carries the subject's own quote, with the wildfire wording of the form spelled out.

Fire and Flood

Three fire declarations in the summer of 2025, the Hawk Fire emergency of 2026 and the flood disaster of 2023.

Every federal declaration for Nevada in 2025 was a fire management assistance grant: the Marie Fire on June 10 (FM-5586-NV), the Conner Fire in Douglas County on June 20 (FM-5592-NV) and the Peavine Fire on August 2 (FM-5602-NV) 19. The Conner Fire's mapped area is in the Lake Tahoe section.

In 2026 the Hawk Fire in Washoe County, burning from August 22 to September 9, brought a presidential emergency declaration, EM-3653-NV, on September 1, with Public Assistance Category B at a 75% federal share 20. An OpenFEMA-derived tracker lists one more fire management declaration, FM-5678, on October 2, 2026 21.

Nevada's most recent major disaster for flooding is DR-4708-NV, the severe winter storms and flooding of March 2023 22. This record holds no county hazard ranking from the National Risk Index, nor any seismic or flood exposure measure, so the dated declarations above are the hazard evidence a Nevada file can cite.

Reno

Reno and Sparks: Washoe County figures inside a metro the register draws as Washoe, Storey and Lyon counties.

The register's Reno market is the federal Reno metro, Lyon, Storey and Washoe counties. The population counts here are Washoe County's and its two cities'; the payroll and broker figures are DETR's and the brokers' Reno area readings; and Lyon County's counts are in the Fernley section.

Washoe County held 519,471 residents on July 1, 2025 by the Department of Taxation's Governor-certified estimates; its 2020 Census count was 486,492 and the state's figure for July 2020 was 473,606, while Reno held 283,378 and Sparks 117,116 8. Cushman & Wakefield's first-quarter 2026 report gives the Reno area an employment base of 281,900 jobs and an unemployment rate of 4.2% 35, and DETR's August 2026 count of Reno-Sparks payroll jobs was 2,800 above the year-earlier figure 4, with the metro's unemployment rate at 3.5% before seasonal adjustment 36.

Apartments are tight. NVBEX's second-quarter 2026 count was 54,345 units, up from 53,565 twelve months before, with 455 units absorbed in the quarter 37. Colliers put vacancy at 3.0% that quarter, down from 3.9% a year before 38, and Marcus & Millichap expects 2026 deliveries at less than one-fifth of 2025's 39. Brokers split the apartment market into South, North, Central and East Reno: new inventory is concentrated in Central Reno, and Class A vacancy is lowest in South Reno and Sparks 3839. A second-quarter 2026 asking rent was not found.

Warehouses are read two ways. Colliers' second-quarter 2026 report puts industrial vacancy at 11.5%, asking rents of $0.80 a square foot, net absorption of 2.04 million square feet and $650 million of institutional capital deployed in the first half of the year 40. CBRE's reading in the same quarter, as Nevada Business's ReD Report gives it, has vacancy falling into single digits for the first time since the second quarter of 2024 41. The two brokers disagree, and a study names the one it uses. Most of the north's data centers sit near the Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center 42, and Reno's 2026 moratorium is in the power section.

The latest hotel figures in the record are for the fiscal year to June 30, 2024, when the Reno-Sparks Convention and Visitors Authority reported 63.5% occupancy, an average cash room rate of $147.50 and 3,156,493 occupied room nights in Washoe County 43. Its room tax has stood since July 1, 2009 at 13.0% in Reno outside downtown, 13.5% downtown and in Sparks, and 13.0% in unincorporated Washoe 43. Unincorporated Washoe licenses short-term rentals under a 2021 ordinance, amended in 2022, that limits occupancy by permitted bedroom and issues permits to owners only 44.

Lake Tahoe, Nevada

Lake Tahoe's Nevada shore, Douglas and Washoe counties only: capped vacation rentals, a hospital's Certificate of Need hearing and the Conner Fire.

This section uses Douglas and Washoe counties and nothing from the California shore, which lies outside this page; Washoe County's figures are in the Reno section. No population for the Nevada side of the basin was found, so Douglas County stands in for it: 57,149 residents in the certified estimate for July 1, 2025, against 49,488 in the 2020 Census 8.

The lodging nodes the record names are the Stateline casino corridor, Zephyr Cove, Glenbrook, and Incline Village and Crystal Bay, the last two in Washoe County 45. Vacation rental supply on the south shore is set by rule: Douglas County caps vacation home rental permits in its Tahoe Township, with density limits by building type, under an ordinance in force since mid-2021 45. The county's revisions keep vacation rentals out of the county outside the Tahoe Basin, cap daytime occupancy in the larger permit tiers against the nighttime limit and require a professional manager for rentals with the highest occupancy limits 46. In Incline Village and Crystal Bay, Washoe's rules limit occupancy by permitted bedroom and issue permits only to owners 44. Hotel occupancy and room rates for the Nevada shore were not found, and the Douglas County lodging tax for Tahoe has no primary-source confirmation.

The state held a Certificate of Need public hearing on Barton Health's replacement hospital at Stateline on February 16, 2024 47.

The Conner Fire, declared on June 20, 2025, burned in Douglas County; the county's emergency management office mapped it at 17,714 acres in its update of June 23 19. The record does not place the fire inside the Tahoe Basin, so a basin site reads its own fire history from the county. NV Energy's notice to Liberty Utilities, in the power section, concerns customers on the California shore; the California page carries no Tahoe section.

Carson City

Carson City, a consolidated city and county: certified counts to 2025 and a payroll count for August 2026.

Carson City is its own county, and the register reads it on that one county. Its certified count for July 1, 2025 is 61,888, 2.7% more than a year before; the 2020 Census had 58,639 and the state's estimate for July 2020 56,434 8.

DETR's Carson City metro count for August 2026 was 32.3 thousand nonfarm jobs, down 1.2% from August 2025 4, with unemployment at 3.4% before seasonal adjustment 36. The capital also houses one of the Nevada District's two SBA offices (see the SBA section).

No asset-class count, employer headcount or 2025 permit figure for Carson City was found. The nearest lodging series is the Gaming Control Board's monthly reporting for its Carson Valley area, which joins Carson City with Gardnerville, Minden and Douglas County outside South Lake Tahoe 48, so a hotel study in the capital builds its own competitive set.

Fernley and Lyon County

Fernley and Lyon County, read at the county: certified counts, and a data center that won its rezoning.

The figures here are Lyon County's. The county had 66,154 residents on July 1, 2025 by the certified estimate, up from 59,235 at the 2020 Census and 57,629 in July 2020 8. Lyon is also one of the register's three Reno metro counties. A nonfarm payroll change for Lyon County was not found, and neither were its employers, apartments, hotels or retail in a published source.

Lyon County's story in 2025 and 2026 is land for data centers. Copia Power's Monarch Data Center near Yerington, planned at 1,000 MW, had its site changed from Agriculture to Specific Plan on December 4, 2025, with its power depending on NV Energy's Greenlink transmission and the Walker River substation 25, and the county commissioners approved the rezoning 3 to 1 on August 6, 2026, with design approval and permits still to come 3316. In August 2026 the county was drafting a data-center ordinance that would require a conditional use permit in every zoning district where the use is allowed 42, and a renewable energy ordinance passed in June 2025 sets setbacks for solar and battery projects 16.

The campus's water demand is reported in units that conflict, between NVBEX's own figure and the county agenda documents it quotes 49, so a study of a site near it reads the county's own record. One of the four abatements GOED approved in February 2026, in the economy section, went to Comstock Metals in Lyon County. A retail or service project in Fernley or Dayton is read on these county figures until a trade-area count is built.

Elko

Elko County: gold, the county's towns in the certified estimates, and a December gaming month.

The register's Elko market adds Eureka County to Elko County; every figure in this section is Elko County's or one of its towns'. Elko County's certified count for July 1, 2025 is 58,031, beside 53,702 at the 2020 Census and the state's 55,435 for July 2020 8. The city of Elko held 22,123, against 20,564 in the 2020 Census; West Wendover 4,486, Carlin 2,498 and Wells 1,330 8.

Gold is the economy, and its jobs are hard to count from published lists. DETR's 2026 Elko list puts Barrick Goldstrike, Newmont and Empire Cat each at 100 to 249 at their Elko establishments 50, a reading that leaves out the workers at the mines themselves. Mine-level employee counts, company and contract staff by mine, are in the Division of Minerals' Major Mines of Nevada, whose 2024 edition was not retrieved for this page 51.

The county's lodging has no occupancy series. Its gaming win, the proxy the record holds, was $34,256,904 in December 2024, down 5.95% on the December before, by the Gaming Control Board's report of January 30, 2025 48. That is one month, from 2024, so a hotel or RV park study in Elko County asks the operators for their own months. No 2025 permit count, nonfarm change or local rule for the county was found.

Winnemucca and Humboldt County

Winnemucca and Humboldt County: county counts, Thacker Pass and the workforce hub built for it.

The population figures here are Humboldt County's; none is Winnemucca's own. The county counted 17,918 residents in the certified estimate for July 1, 2025; the 2020 Census found 17,285, and the state's July 2020 estimate was 17,064 8.

Thacker Pass is the project that moves the county. Lithium Americas reported its first drawdown of $435 million under its Department of Energy loan for Phase 1 in 2025, with Bechtel managing engineering, procurement and construction under a project labor agreement with the building trades 52. To house the construction crews it built a workforce hub in east Winnemucca, which took its first residents in late September 2025; the company said in October 2025 that local hotel rooms were opening up as workers moved into the hub 24. The county adopted a moratorium on new data-center approvals in 2026 16.

For a lodging, apartment or RV project, the hub is the competitor to account for. No occupancy figure for Winnemucca hotels, no employer list with headcounts, no nonfarm change and no asset-class count for the county was found, so the study builds its demand from the project's own construction schedule and the hub's published plans.

Laughlin

Laughlin, an unincorporated Clark County town: its own resident, visitor and room-rate figures.

Laughlin is a town inside Clark County, so its figures are kept apart from the county figures on the Las Vegas page, and none is counted twice. The town's certified count was 8,810 on July 1, 2025; the 2020 Census had found 9,971, and the July 2020 estimate put it at 10,306 8.

The Review-Journal, reporting the visitor authority's year-end figures in January 2026, put Laughlin at 1.4 million visitors in 2025, 7.3% more than in 2024, with hotels 48% full and rooms averaging $62.13 a night 53. The room count did not change during the year, by the same report, and no RevPAR or published room total for the town was found. The visitor authority's monthly summaries carry a Laughlin panel, with passengers at the Laughlin/Bullhead City airport and traffic on Highway 163, but those values were not retrieved 54.

The record holds no local rule for Laughlin beyond Clark County's unincorporated code, which was not retrieved. A resort, RV or retail study here reads the casino operators' own records beside those visitor and rate figures.

Payrolls and Employers

August 2026 payrolls, the construction count, the state's largest employers and two rounds of GOED abatements.

DETR's August 2026 count of 1,619,400 nonfarm jobs was 20,700 above August 2025, a gain of 1.3%, and 4,400 above July 4. Over that one month leisure and hospitality added 3,100 jobs and professional and business services 1,000, while trade, transportation and utilities lost 1,500 4. A month earlier, DETR's July report had professional services and health care each growing more than 4% over the year 55. Construction employment stood at 116,800 in August, 3,600 more than a year before, in NVBEX's reading of DETR's figures 56. A twelve-month change for each supersector was not found.

DETR's 2026 statewide list is drawn from an establishment database and gives ranges, not headcounts. Among its private employers, nine sit in the 5,000 to 9,999 band: Flamingo Las Vegas, MGM Grand, the Orleans, Las Vegas Sands, Mandalay Bay, Caesars Palace, the Aquarius Casino Resort in Laughlin, Tesla's Gigafactory Nevada in Storey County and Planet Hollywood. Harveys Lake Tahoe, in Douglas County, is tenth at 1,000 to 4,999 57.

Two rounds of abatements show where manufacturing and data-center capital went. On February 5, 2026 GOED approved abatements of $27,176,749 for four companies, Amperesand in Reno, Future Form in Sparks, Comstock Metals in Lyon County and a data center in Storey County, which together plan $340,776,410 of capital equipment investment and 352 jobs 28. In August 2026 GOED approved nine companies' ten projects in Clark, Washoe, Storey and Lyon counties, in manufacturing, data centers, packaging and steel: nearly $153 million of investment after approximately $17.6 million of abatements, projected to generate $174.2 million of new tax revenue 34. No complete register of 2025 and 2026 projects above $100 million was found, so a study near one of these sites confirms its status with the county.

Licensing and Local Rules

Certificate of Need in the small counties, Clark County's rental licences in court, and the Petroleum Fund's books.

Certificate of Need

Under NRS 439A.100 a health facility needs the Director of Health and Human Services to approve new construction spending above the statutory threshold, but only in the state's smaller counties and in small cities and towns inside the large ones, so most of Clark and Washoe counties fall outside it 61. The Barton Health hearing in the Lake Tahoe section is the recent precedent. Assisted living and memory care licence categories could not be confirmed from a primary source, so an assisted living study reads NRS Chapter 449 directly; child care licensing classes were not found either.

Short-term rentals

AB 363 of 2021 required Clark County and its larger cities to license short-term rentals; it is a mandate to license, not a state preemption. Clark County's own ordinance of June 21, 2022, and the court fight over its enforcement, are in the Las Vegas page's rules section 6226. The Washoe and Douglas rules are in the Reno and Lake Tahoe sections.

Fuel tanks

The Nevada Petroleum Fund under NRS 445C is funded by a fee of $0.0075 per gallon and an enrollment fee of $100 per tank. At the Board to Review Claims meeting of March 12, 2026, the fund had carried $7,500,000 forward from fiscal 2025 and collected about $5,011,701 in fees and about $415,925 in enrollment fees so far in fiscal 2026; it had opened 1,895 cleanup cases since its start and closed 1,648 11. The minutes are a draft. The fund pays for corrective action on a release, less the owner's co-payment, but not for removing or upgrading a tank 63; a separate state grant program helps sites with smaller fuel throughput upgrade their tank systems 64. No state licensing rule for RV parks or campgrounds and no liquor licence quota was confirmed.

Taxes and Wages

Nevada's sales tax, a homeowner property tax benchmark, one statewide minimum wage and public works wages after Stuckey.

Tax Foundation's 2026 edition gives the state sales tax rate as 6.85% and the average combined state and local rate as 8.24% 65; how construction materials and business equipment are treated was not found. Its effective property tax rate of 0.50% covers owner-occupied housing only, from 2024 survey data 65, and no effective rate for commercial property was found, so a commercial pro forma takes the county assessor's figure for the parcel. Nevada also caps how fast a property tax bill can rise each year, with one cap for primary residences and another for commercial and other property, by a secondary calculator's account 66.

Nevada has had one minimum wage for all employees since July 1, 2024, set under the state constitution, with no tip credit and daily overtime below a set hourly rate, by an employers' association's 2026 summary; the Labor Commissioner's bulletin was not retrieved 67.

Public works over $100,000 pay prevailing wages under NRS 338, in four wage regions, and the current determinations apply to projects bid or awarded from October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026 68. On February 26, 2026 the Nevada Supreme Court held in Stuckey v. Apex Materials that the chapter gives no private right to sue, so claims go through the Labor Commissioner 29. On April 17, 2026 the U.S. Department of Labor adopted the state's rates for federal Davis-Bacon projects, back to the October 1, 2025 rates 30. Nevada Questions

Nevada feasibility study questions.

What did NV Energy tell Liberty Utilities about Lake Tahoe?

That about 75% of the power for Liberty's California customers at the lake stops by May 2027, as RCG Economics reported in July 2026, with the power going to data centers instead 1. The customers are Californians; a Nevada shore project reads its own service terms from NV Energy.

Can a Nevada property policy leave out wildfire?

Yes, since January 1, 2026. AB 376, signed in June 2025, lets a carrier exclude wildfire or sell it as a standalone policy, subject to the Division of Insurance approving the form and the rate 3. A commercial file quotes the subject's own policy, wildfire clause included.

Which 2025 population does Nevada certify, and which does the Census Bureau publish?

Nevada certifies 3,349,303 residents for July 1, 2025, a 2.0% rise on the year, in the estimate the Department of Taxation publishes under the Governor's certification 8. The Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 estimate for the same date is 3,282,188 7, 0.9% above its July 1, 2024 figure 27. Every county, city and town count on this page comes from the certified series.

Where in Reno are the new apartments, and how full is the market?

The new buildings sit in Central Reno, and South Reno and Sparks hold the lowest Class A vacancy, in the brokers' South, North, Central and East Reno split 3839. NVBEX counted 54,345 units in the second quarter of 2026, up from 53,565 twelve months before, 455 of them absorbed in the quarter 37. Colliers' vacancy reading was 3.0%, against 3.9% a year earlier 38, and Marcus & Millichap's forecast puts 2026 deliveries under one-fifth of 2025's 39.

What does a data center need for Nevada's 20-year tax abatement?

GOED's August 2026 terms ask for 50 full-time employees who live in Nevada, wages at 100% of the statewide average, and capital spending of $100 million inside five years, against a sales and use tax rate cut to 2% 10.

What has the Thacker Pass workforce hub done to Winnemucca's hotel rooms?

Opened them up. Lithium Americas built the hub in east Winnemucca for its construction crews; it took its first residents in late September 2025, and in October 2025 the company said local hotel rooms were freeing as workers moved in 24. In 2025 the company drew the first $435 million of its Department of Energy loan for Phase 1 52. No occupancy figure for Winnemucca hotels was found, so a lodging study there sizes demand from the mine's build schedule and what the company has published about the hub.
