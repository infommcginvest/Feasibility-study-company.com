An LNG decision in Calcasieu, a steel mill in Ascension, and data center campuses in Richland and Rapides parishes.

Four dated projects, and the population estimates of the metros around them.

Woodside took its final investment decision on Louisiana LNG in Calcasieu Parish on April 29, 2025, with first LNG targeted for 2029 15; Hyundai Steel broke ground on its Ascension Parish mill in September 2026, with production set for 2029 16; Meta's Richland Parish campus was announced in December 2024 and expanded in July 2026 9117; and Applied Digital announced Delta Forge 1 in Rapides Parish on May 26, 2026 62.

Baton Rouge

Baton Rouge, with Ascension, Pointe Coupee and West Feliciana among its outer parishes: payrolls, apartments and new projects.

Workforce Commission payrolls, an April 2026 apartment reading and the outer parishes' projects.

The Workforce Commission counted 438,800 nonfarm jobs in the Baton Rouge metro in August 2026, up 13,700, or 3.22%, over twelve months 67. KNOE's report of the Vintage 2025 estimates puts the metro at 888,699 residents against an April 2020 count of 870,569 18, and the City of Baton Rouge at 222,795 in July 2025 75. The employer record behind those jobs is old: HUD's 2010 market analysis listed LSU at 15,550 employees in total and 6,150 full time, Turner Industries at 8,325 and ExxonMobil at 4,275 76.

The new projects sit in the outer parishes. Hyundai Steel broke ground on its Ascension Parish mill in September 2026 with production set for 2029 16, after Entergy Louisiana and the company executed an electric service agreement in May 61. The Hut 8 River Bend AI campus in West Feliciana Parish, leased to Fluidstack, was under construction in 2026 77, and Pointe Coupee Parish is one of the two parishes in the gas plant package the Public Service Commission fast-tracked in April 2026 60. A broker's list of the city's largest new-construction projects of 2025, drawn from city open data, includes an apartment complex permitted in April 2025 78.

Apartment vacancy is on record here. Local press, quoting appraiser data at an April 2026 seminar, put multifamily vacancy at 6.53%, down from 7.46%, with asking rent of about $1.34 per square foot, and the presenter warned that effective vacancy could approach 12.5% once offline distressed units are counted 79. Cushman and Wakefield counted 38,582 units of inventory in Q3 2025 80. The two come from different publishers and dates, a vacancy rate in April 2026 and an inventory count in Q3 2025.

New Orleans

New Orleans, Orleans Parish and the wider metro: hotel months, rental zoning and the estimates.

Event-driven lodging, the city's rental rules, and the parish and metro estimates.

CoStar recorded New Orleans hotel occupancy of 53.9% in July 2025, the lowest of the Top 25 markets 69. During the 2025 Super Bowl week the market reached 92.9% weekend occupancy at an average rate of $486 70. February 2026 showed an average rate of $204.95, 33.7% below February 2025, the Super Bowl month, with RevPAR of $136.00 71; August 2026 brought 43.3% occupancy and RevPAR of $51.63 72.

KNOE, reporting the Census Vintage 2025 estimates, put the New Orleans metro at 970,849 residents in July 2025 against an April 2020 count of 1,007,275 18. The Data Center reports a net loss of 36,426 since 2020 on the same 2025 total 65. The Data Center also counts 21,228 Hispanic residents added since 2020 65. The city itself, which is Orleans Parish, held 362,154 people in July 2025 against an estimates base of 384,129 66. The metro carried 470,300 nonfarm jobs in August 2026 67. The latest employer list found dates from 2012, with Ochsner at 9,107 employees and St. Tammany schools at 7,651 68.

The city stopped accepting new commercial short-term rental applications in June 2023 19, removed every exception to its one-per-square density rule in March 2025 56, and required platforms to verify permits from August 2025 58. With the prior moratorium due to expire in November 2025 73, the Council adopted a Transient Lodging Interim Zoning District on May 7, 2026, making new hotels, hostels, commercial rentals, timeshares and bed and breakfasts conditional uses 20. The Fifth Circuit struck the business-entity ban in October 2025 21 and upheld the non-commercial ordinance in August 2026 22. Act 387 of 2025 also amended the state's New Orleans rental provisions 74. Room charges carry state and city sales tax plus several occupancy levies 58; the combined rate was not found.

Capital Access

SBA loan files, an SSBCI allocation of up to $113 million and a conduit bond authority.

SBA's shutdown estimate and its files for fiscal 2016 to 2025, LEDC's credit programs and the Public Facilities Authority's 2025 issuance.

During the October 2025 federal shutdown SBA estimated that $7,243,056 of Louisiana 7(a) and 504 lending was blocked each week 31.

FY2025 state approvals by program were not found. Two CDCs, JEDCO and Louisiana Capital, offer SBA 504 financing, and JEDCO states that it can serve a business anywhere in Louisiana 32; 504 debentures approved for Louisiana projects in fiscal years 2016 to 2025, net of cancellations, total $186.0 million in SBA's FOIA files 30.

The state's own credit programs

A Small Business Loan Guaranty Program was active in November 2025, when the Louisiana Economic Development Corporation's board agenda carried a new CDFI lender applicant 35, and LEDC's SSBCI credit programs include a micro-lending revolving fund delivered through community development financial institutions 34. The SSBCI award itself leans to equity: of up to $113 million for Louisiana, up to $91.5 million is set aside for venture and seed capital programs, with LEDC as administrator 33. A separate state capital access program was not found.

Conduit bonds for health care and education

The Louisiana Public Facilities Authority's annual report, sponsored content, lists 2025 bond issues for Ochsner and for LSU's South Quad 36. The State Bond Commission gave final approval on April 22, 2025 to authority revenue and refunding bonds for Ochsner not exceeding $1,035,000,000 37.

Louisiana's gate for care uses is Facility Need Review, not a certificate of need 10. Every provider type in the table passes it before first licensure, among them adult residential care, the license behind assisted living, and psychosocial rehabilitation, a behavioral health service 10. The state's residents aged 65 and over numbered 772,404 in the five-year American Community Survey for 2020 to 2024 28. For nursing facilities, R.S. 40:2116.1 adds a moratorium, with exceptions for a replacement that adds no beds and for nursing beds converted to adult residential care 11. The review rule refers to adult residential care level 4 40; definitions of the levels and bed thresholds were not found.

Louisiana has no statute that enables or preempts local short-term rental rules: HB 469, which would have written local authority into statute, died on June 12, 2025 46, and SB 225, creating a private right of action against unpermitted rentals, reached the reengrossed stage, and its final status was not found 47. Act 387 of 2025 amended the state's New Orleans rental provisions 74, and the city writes its own rules. Louisiana's minimum wage in 2026 follows the federal rate of $7.25 an hour, parishes and cities are barred from setting their own under R.S. 23:642, and HB 353, which would have set $12 rising to $15, was rejected 7 to 5 by the House Labor Committee on April 9, 2026; it is not law 48.

A gas station or travel center sits under DEQ's Motor Fuels Underground Storage Tank Trust Fund, set up by Act 767 of 1988, and under R.S. 30:2195.4 its cleanup costs must be approved in advance to be reimbursed 43. The Legislative Auditor audits the fund's dedicated account and its transfers for FY2024 45. DEQ's separate upgrade grant takes up to 20 owners a year 49.

Child care centers are licensed under BESE Bulletin 137 as Type I, II or III, and a Type III center also needs academic approval under Bulletin 140 41. The bulletin sets staff-to-child ratios by age band and requires at least two staff whenever more than four children are present 41.

Louisiana Questions

Louisiana feasibility study and market study questions.

How did Alexandria's payrolls move over the year to August 2026?

Down 0.16% over the twelve months, to 62,200 nonfarm jobs in August 2026, by the Workforce Commission's count 67. KNOE's report of the Vintage 2025 estimates puts the metro at 147,952 residents in July 2025 18.

Which New Orleans and Lafayette figures on this page are parish counts?

Orleans Parish, which is also the city of New Orleans, stood at 362,154 residents in July 2025; its estimates base was 384,129 66. Lafayette Parish stood at 257,949 in July 2025, from 254,674 twelve months before 81. For Houma-Thibodaux the federal wage record is kept by parish as well: Terrebonne had 47,851 covered jobs at an average weekly wage of $1,262 in the fourth quarter of 2024, and Lafourche 35,395 jobs at $1,353 89.

Which Louisiana care providers pass Facility Need Review, and which sit under a moratorium?

The Department of Health applies Facility Need Review before first licensure to nursing facilities, adult residential care, hospice, adult day health care, psychosocial rehabilitation and other listed provider types 10. Nursing facilities are also under a statutory moratorium, R.S. 40:2116.1, which a replacement facility with no added beds, or a conversion of nursing beds to adult residential care, does not breach 11, and intermediate care facilities for the developmentally disabled have a moratorium of their own 10.

How is a child care center licensed in Louisiana?

By BESE under Bulletin 137, which divides centers into Type I, Type II and Type III; a Type III center also carries academic approval under Bulletin 140 41. The same bulletin fixes staff-to-child ratios for each age band and calls for two staff members on duty once more than four children are in care 41.

Can a New Orleans hotel or short-term rental feasibility study count on steady lodging income?

The record argues against assuming it. CoStar's July 2025 release put New Orleans hotel occupancy at 53.9%, the lowest of the Top 25 markets 69; Super Bowl week in February 2025 ran to 92.9% occupancy on the weekend, with an average rate of $486 70; August 2026 showed 43.3% occupancy and RevPAR of $51.63 72. Permission is the other variable: New Orleans has taken no new commercial short-term rental applications since June 2023 19.

What is Bossier City spending on roads and capital projects?

Its five-year capital plan for 2026 to 2030 runs to more than $188 million 85. State capital outlay funds the Benton Road and I-220 interchange and the Barksdale Boulevard widening 86, and a U.S. 71 rehabilitation in the Shreveport area was due to start the week of September 28, 2026 64.

Does Louisiana levy a state property tax?

No. None has been imposed since 1972, although the constitution allows one of up to 5.75 mills 50; a Louisiana site pays parish and municipal millage instead.
