The State Read

A benchmarked payroll count, a recut credit program and record homeowner non-renewals.

Much of what changed for New Mexico projects between January 2025 and October 2026 changed in a document first. The Department of Workforce Solutions benchmarked its 2025 payroll count in January 2026 1; the New Mexico Finance Authority reallocated part of the state's SSBCI money in September 2025 5; and the Superintendent of Insurance told legislators in July 2026 that homeowner non-renewals had set a record in 2025 14. Each is set out below with its date.

Federal work runs through the state's markets. Two national laboratories, both contractor-operated, sit in the top band of the state's unofficial list of private employers 15, and the market sections add a missile range at Las Cruces and a spaceport near Truth or Consequences. The July 2026 review shows federal government employment down on the year 2.

The markets differ most in what their records hold. Albuquerque is the only one with broker series for apartments and industrial space; Los Alamos is read on a laboratory's budget, Truth or Consequences on a spaceport's impact report, and the Permian Basin on city and county accounts.

Payrolls

The 2025 benchmark, the July 2026 payroll review and the projects the state's development department counted.

In its January 2026 article on the benchmark review, the Department of Workforce Solutions revised calendar 2025 job growth to 3,200 jobs, a gain of 0.4%, and found the last three months of the year negative 1. That revised figure is the one this page uses for 2025; the department's earlier estimate is superseded by it and is not printed. The same article revised the Santa Fe and Farmington metro counts, which their sections give.

The department's Labor Market Review for July 2026 counted 887,900 nonfarm jobs, not seasonally adjusted, 6,700 or 0.8% more than a year earlier. Private employers added 6,000 jobs (0.9%) and the public sector 700 (0.4%), while federal government employment fell by 2,300, or 8.0% 2. The review's table of changes by industry was not read for this page, so no sector is named as the source of the private gain.

The state's largest private employers are published only in size bands. The Employment & Economic Information Center's list, compiled in August 2025 for the NM Career Development Association and marked unofficial, puts Presbyterian Healthcare Services and Walmart in its top band with Los Alamos and Sandia National Laboratories, and Albertsons, Amazon, Intel's Rio Rancho plant, Lovelace Hospitals and Smith's Foods in the band below 15.

Two defense manufacturing announcements in the Economic Development Department's Pathfinder newsletter sit in the market sections: a rocket motor plant in Sandoval County, under Rio Rancho, and an expansion in Bernalillo County, under Albuquerque. The State Site Readiness Program, in force from July 1, 2025, had evaluated 47 sites in its first six months by the newsletter of January 15, 2026 16.

Costs, Taxes and Insurance

Record homeowner non-renewals, a wider FAIR Plan, the gross receipts tax by location and the 2026 public works wages.

The Superintendent of Insurance told the Legislative Finance Committee on July 21, 2026 that insurers declined to renew more than 6,200 New Mexico homeowner policies in 2025, a record, against about 2,200 in 2021; Rio Arriba, Taos, Sandoval and Santa Fe counties were the most at risk, and most of the non-renewals came in wildfire-prone areas 14. Average premium increases were under 5% in 2025 and 2026, after 9.3% in 2024 and 15% in 2023 14.

The state's insurer of last resort, the New Mexico Property Insurance Program, raised its residential limit from $350,000 to $750,000 by a Governing Committee vote of February 20, 2025 25. From October 15, 2025, by Source NM's report, businesses in disaster areas could buy commercial cover of up to $2 million from it, against $1 million before; the program then insured 7,200 residents and 280 businesses 26. A $10 million appropriation for wildfire mitigation grants to its policyholders was due to expire on June 30, 2026, and the superintendent asked legislators to extend what Source NM called the nation's largest wildfire mitigation program 27.

The gross receipts tax is set by location. On the Taxation and Revenue Department's schedule for July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026 the state rate is 4.875%, Albuquerque's combined rate 7.625%, and the highest combined rate listed 10.8125%, in Española and the Santa Clara Grant 7.

Public works funded by the state or a local government pay the prevailing wage. The 2026 Type B rates, in effect from January 1, 2026, set a Group I laborer at $22.37 an hour plus $8.48 in fringe, against $20.44 plus $7.96 in 2025, and a carpenter at $32.12 plus $14.28, against $30.89 plus $13.26 28. No effective commercial property tax rate for the state was found.

Hazards

Lincoln County's 2025 flood disaster, the counties SBA added to it, and what flood cover costs in New Mexico.

FEMA-4886-DR was declared on July 22, 2025 for severe storms, flooding and landslides in an incident period from June 23 to August 5, 2025, with Lincoln County as its primary county 8. SBA's notice opened its economic injury disaster loans, and no other assistance, to Chaves, De Baca, Guadalupe, Otero, Sierra, Socorro and Torrance counties 9. The Rio Ruidoso reached a record stage of 20 feet, five feet above its prior high, and the preliminary count was 35 to 50 homes damaged or lost 6.

Lincoln and Otero counties held 1,223 flood insurance policies under the national program in 2025, and the state's average flood premium was $951 a year, which the same report puts $184 above the national average 6. Sierra County, one of the contiguous counties, is the Truth or Consequences section's county. No federal disaster declaration for New Mexico in 2026 was found, and no reading of the state's wind or seismic exposure.

Albuquerque

Albuquerque: two apartment readings, two industrial readings and the hotel year to July 2026.

The register's Albuquerque metro is four counties, Bernalillo, Sandoval, Torrance and Valencia, and Sandoval County's figures sit in the Rio Rancho section, so that none is counted twice.

Bernalillo County's Vintage 2025 estimate is 667,601 for July 1, 2025, on an April 2020 base of 676,446 (the table's change is -1.3%), and 1,560 units of housing were permitted in the county in 2025 32. Retail sales in Bernalillo County were $20,770 per resident in the 2022 Economic Census 33.

Federal research, defense, the university and the health systems carry the county's payroll. The New Mexico Partnership's list, which gives bands and no date, puts Sandia National Laboratories, Albuquerque Public Schools, the University of New Mexico, UNM Hospital and the City of Albuquerque in its top band, and names the university, UNM Hospital, Presbyterian and Lovelace as the county's university and medical anchors 34. Kirtland Air Force Base's headcount and economic impact reached the research without a source address, so neither is printed. AeroVironment's defense manufacturing expansion in the county, more than $30 million with 450 jobs, is in the Pathfinder of March 6, 2026 35.

Apartments

Colliers put Albuquerque multifamily occupancy at 94.9% in the first quarter of 2026, 0.5 points lower than a year before, with 619 units under construction, 509 of them due within four quarters, and 683 units delivered in the year to that quarter 36. Yardi Matrix's September 2026 report reads 94.3% through July 2026, 30 basis points lower on the year, and an average asking rent of $1,402, down 0.8% 37. The two readings come from different firms and different months, and are not combined here.

Industrial

CBRE's figures for the second quarter of 2026 put Albuquerque industrial vacancy at 3.6%, against 4.4% a year earlier 38. Colliers' first-quarter report reads 4.46%, calls speculative construction negligible and records Array Technologies' solar plant of 215,000 square feet as completed 39.

Rio Rancho

Rio Rancho and Sandoval County: the city's own count, the county's permits and an impact fee schedule written year by year.

Rio Rancho is read on the city's Census count and on Sandoval County, one of the four counties of the register's Albuquerque metro; every Sandoval County figure on this page is here, and only here.

The Census Bureau estimates the city at 114,419 on July 1, 2025 and 104,073 at its April 2020 base, +9.9% by its own table, with retail sales of $8,891 a head in the 2022 Economic Census 33. Sandoval County stood at 159,565 against 148,833 (+7.2%) and permitted 1,019 units of housing in 2025; QuickFacts gives no permit figure for the city itself 43.

The New Mexico Partnership counts Intel's Rio Rancho plant with the Albuquerque area's biggest employers but gives it no headcount 34. In Sandoval County, Castelion's Project Ranger, a solid rocket motor plant, carries $220 million of private investment and 300 jobs in the Pathfinder of February 6, 2026 44; the Albuquerque Regional Economic Alliance named it the state's collaborative impact project of the year on September 10, 2026, after a January 2026 groundbreaking, with production targeted for the end of 2026 45. No broker report in the research breaks out a Rio Rancho submarket for any asset class.

The city's code sets impact fees by year. For the smallest meter the Year 3 schedule, in force from July 1, 2026, charges $4,006.50 for water and $2,544 for sewer, and the Year 4 step from July 1, 2027 raises them to $4,442 and $2,820.50 10. Water and wastewater service charges, volume charges and water-rights fees have risen 3% a year since July 1, 2025 46. A Rio Rancho project's development budget takes the fee year in which it connects, and its operating budget carries utility charges that rise each July.

Santa Fe

Santa Fe County: the capital's payroll, a hotel reopened under a new name, and lodging taxed by both the city and the county.

Santa Fe County's July 2025 estimate, 156,907, sits on an April 2020 base of 154,831 (+1.3%), and 495 units of housing were permitted there in 2025 47. On the workforce department's benchmarked count, the Santa Fe metro added 200 jobs in 2025, 0.3% 1.

The New Mexico Partnership's list names the State of New Mexico, Christus St. Vincent Regional Medical Center and the City of Santa Fe among the county's largest employers, without a count for the state 34. Hotel Glorieta, the former Lodge at Santa Fe, reopened with 128 rooms on November 12, 2025 after a repositioning 48. No dated hotel occupancy, average rate or revenue per room for the county was found. Cerrillos Road is the chain-hotel corridor on record: the Santa Fe New Mexican reported two hotels opening on one block of it in 2020 49.

The city levies a lodgers' tax and a separate convention center fee, and a short-term rental needs a permit in residential zoning and a registration and business licence elsewhere 50. The county brought short-term rentals into its own lodgers' tax by Ordinance No. 2023-02 and licenses owner-occupied and other rentals separately 51. The research holds no multifamily, storage, industrial or senior housing reading for the county.

Los Alamos

Los Alamos County: the laboratory's 2026 appropriations and hiring plan, and the housing shortage reported around it.

Los Alamos National Laboratory expects $5.2 billion of federal appropriations in 2026 and plans 1,000 to 1,400 hires, about 900 of them replacing staff who leave, in the Los Alamos Reporter's account, on March 5, 2026, of a briefing to the county council 3. The Reporter's headline ties a significant portion of the appropriations to expanding nuclear weapons work. No official headcount for the laboratory was found.

Los Alamos County numbered 19,407 people in the July 2025 estimate and 19,416 at the April 2020 base, a change the Census table gives as 0.0%, and permitted 15 units of housing in 2025 52. The Santa Fe New Mexican's report on the laboratory's fiscal 2026 hiring says summer interns camped in the forests near the lab and that student housing is a stated laboratory priority 53.

Los Alamos is not one of the workforce department's metro areas, and no county job change was found; nor were supply readings, local rules or any figure for Rio Arriba County. A project here is read on the laboratory's budget and hiring and on the county's two Census counts.

Las Cruces

Las Cruces and Doña Ana County: a missile range, a payroll gain since 2022 and a data center campus at Santa Teresa.

Las Cruces is read on Doña Ana County, a New Mexico county only and the register's whole market for it. In the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 table the county has 229,091 residents for July 1, 2025, up from an April 2020 base of 219,563 (+4.3%), with 833 units of housing permitted in 2025 54. The workforce department's 2026 State of the Workforce counts 10,325 more nonfarm jobs in the county from January 2022 to June 2026, a gain of 14.4% 55.

HUD's 2022 housing market analysis for Las Cruces counted 5,350 people at White Sands Missile Range, about 5,000 civilians and 350 in uniform, and put the City of Las Cruces at 1,000 to 4,999 employees 56. The New Mexico Partnership's undated list adds New Mexico State University, Las Cruces Public Schools and Memorial Medical Center 34.

At Santa Teresa, Ordinance 367-2025 of September 2025 lets the county issue as much as $165 billion in industrial revenue bonds for Project Jupiter, an AI data center campus with Oracle as tenant, and Ordinance 368-2025 sets its share of gross receipts tax support under the Local Economic Development Act 57. Oracle's announcement of July 28, 2026 includes a $50 million water infrastructure commitment to the county, 80% funded 58. On September 17, 2026 the New Mexico Supreme Court rejected two challenges to the project, one of them to its emergency construction well, while the Environment Department's air permit for its gas fuel-cell system was still pending 59; the Albuquerque Journal reports that Oracle sent a force majeure notice to the developer, Stack Infrastructure, while saying the campus remains on schedule 60. The county has also set up a committee to monitor the project, by KFOX14's report on an update from Oracle and Bloom Energy 61.

NM-136, the Pete V. Domenici Highway, carries the Santa Teresa industrial and data center corridor to the Port of Entry 62, the one border crossing the research names; the port's throughput was not found. Our Texas page follows Project Jupiter from the other side of the state line, in its El Paso section. No supply series for any asset class, lodgers' tax rate or impact fee was found for the county.

Permian Basin

The Permian Basin in New Mexico, Lea and Eddy counties only: county counts, Hobbs's air service budget and a lodgers' tax that no longer reaches long stays.

The two counties' Vintage 2025 estimates are 74,749 for Lea and 62,509 for Eddy, against April 2020 bases of 74,452 and 62,317 (+0.4% and +0.3% in the Census tables); Lea permitted 143 units of housing in 2025 and Eddy 241 units 6667. Oil and gas show in the public accounts. Hobbs budgeted an airline subsidy of $2,720,982 for fiscal year 2025-26, including $225,000 from the lodgers' tax, and $200,000 for its Fly Hobbs air service marketing, in the city commission packet of July 21, 2025, which also carries a single-family development agreement with Habitat for Humanity 70. A study there counts its own competitors, because no newer series stands in for that report.

Farmington

Farmington and San Juan County: a coal plant's retirement, a benchmarked 2025 payroll gain and no supply series on record.

San Juan County, the whole Farmington metro, came to 120,340 people in the Census Bureau's July 2025 estimate, below its April 2020 base of 121,665 (-1.1%), and permitted 80 units of housing in 2025 72. The January 2026 benchmark puts the metro's 2025 payroll gain at 1,400 jobs, or 2.9% 1.

The San Juan Generating Station closed in September 2022, and the San Juan County Commission approved PNM's plan to demolish and remediate it; the same report describes a rail spur from Gallup that the county pursued with the Navajo Nation, with no status given 73. San Juan Regional Medical Center is the one employer named for the county on the New Mexico Partnership's list 34. No supply reading, corridor count, impact fee or rental rule was found for San Juan County.

Rules by Asset Class

An assisted living rule under replacement, child care homes by size, a full liquor quota, a tank fund with new claim rules and an unsettled rental tax class.

Assisted living

Assisted living is licensed by the Health Care Authority under 8.370.14 NMAC, which since July 1, 2024 has applied to any facility caring for two or more adults who need help with one or more activities of daily living 74. The authority held hearings on repealing and replacing it on April 16, 2025 and January 26, 2026 75. Its licensing fee rule, in effect from January 28, 2025, sets an annual base assessment of $300, $300 for each amended licence and $25 a bed for a change of capacity where the fee is per bed 76. No separate memory care licensing category or bed threshold was found.

Child care

Under 8.9.4 NMAC, in its integrated text of November 2025, a family child care home takes up to 6 children and a group home up to 12, with centers above that, and a home needs 35 square feet per child 77. A center's application goes in with its proposed activities and daily schedule, and the licensing authority schedules the initial survey once the application is complete, so the opening date in a child care pro forma follows that survey 78.

Liquor

A secondary summary of the Alcoholic Beverage Control Division's FAQs describes the dispenser and retailer quota as full, so licences for bars and package stores are bought, sold, transferred or leased, while a Restaurant A licence for beer and wine sits outside the quota and cannot be transferred 13. A bar or package store budget therefore carries the cost of buying or leasing a licence from a holder.

Fuel tanks

Underground tanks answer to the Environment Department's Petroleum Storage Tank Bureau under 20.5 NMAC 79. The Environment Department's Corrective Action Fund, paid for by a per-load fee on wholesale petroleum counted in loads of 8,000 gallons, changed its reimbursement-claim requirements on August 21, 2025, and the department says that if the fund's unobligated balance is too low no new cleanup can be started; the balance itself was not found 80.

Short-term rentals

No state law preempts or enables local short-term rental rules. SB 149, which would have classed rentals as residential property, did not advance before the session adjourned on February 19, 2026 81, and HM 62 asks county assessors to pause reclassification until findings due October 1, 2026 4. The local rules that do exist are in the Santa Fe section.

Apartments

Rent control is prohibited statewide under NMSA 47-8A-1, and SB 216, a 2025 bill to repeal the ban, failed, by a secondary summary of the state's rent laws 82. An apartment pro forma in New Mexico therefore sets rent growth from the market, without a local cap to model.

Statewide Supply

New Mexico's short-term rental stock, counted twice by the legislature, and where the broker series sit.

The legislature's HM 62 analysis counted 19,236 short-term rental listings for New Mexico on Airbnb and VRBO combined as of December 2025, 63% of them standalone houses 4. The HM 52 work group's report of December 8, 2025 put the stock at nearly 14,000 rentals statewide, 85% of them seasonal or partially owner-occupied 83. One document counts listings and the other rentals, so the two are given apart.

Albuquerque's lodgers' tax gives a local reading of the same stock: short-term rentals paid 10% of the city's collections in fiscal 2027 to date 12. For apartments, industrial space and hotels, the only broker and visitor bureau series in the research are Albuquerque's, in that section. No statewide count was found for any other asset class.

Checks for the File

What a reviewer holds a New Mexico file against: the county line, the tax period, the cover, the long stay and the rental class.

The side of the county line

Albuquerque and Rio Rancho share a metro, and this page divides it at the line between Bernalillo and Sandoval counties. A site's trade area takes the county counts of its own side, and a trade area that crosses the line takes both sections' figures, each with its own date.

The tax period

The gross receipts rates on this page run from July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026 7. A pro forma that opens later quotes the schedule for its own opening period, at the combined rate for its location rather than the state rate.

Cover in the counties named at risk

For a site in Rio Arriba, Taos, Sandoval or Santa Fe County, the counties the Superintendent of Insurance named as most at risk of non-renewal 14, the file carries a written quote for property cover and says whether the policy is a FAIR Plan policy within the program's limits.

Long stays in the oil counties

A hotel in Lea or Eddy County has collected no lodgers' tax on stays of 30 or more consecutive days since July 1, 2025 69. The file says how much of the hotel's demand is extended-stay oilfield bookings, because those nights now fall outside the tax and outside any lodgers' tax receipts used to size the market.

A rental's tax class

For a short-term rental or a small lodging project the file names the county whose assessor classes the property, because no state law has settled whether rentals are residential or commercial property 4.

A federal site's own record

A project whose demand rests on Los Alamos National Laboratory, White Sands Missile Range or Spaceport America is read on that site's own dated record, whether appropriations and hiring guidance, HUD's 2022 headcount or the flight forecast, and the study states the date each carries.

New Mexico Questions

New Mexico feasibility study questions.

Is the gross receipts tax rate the same across New Mexico?

No. It is set by location. The Taxation and Revenue Department's schedule for the year to June 30, 2026 pairs a 4.875% state rate with combined local rates that reach 10.8125% at the top of its list, for Española and the Santa Clara Grant; Albuquerque's is 7.625% 7.

Does flood cover cost more in New Mexico than the national average?

Yes. The state's average flood insurance premium was $951 a year in 2025, which New Mexico Political Report's account of the Ruidoso flood puts $184 above the national average; Lincoln and Otero counties held 1,223 policies under the national program that year 6.

Do Rio Rancho's impact fees step up each July?

Yes, on a schedule written year by year. On the smallest meter, water is $4,006.50 and sewer $2,544 under Year 3 of the schedule, in force since July 1, 2026; Year 4, from July 1, 2027, puts them at $4,442 and $2,820.50, and larger meters pay more on the same schedule 10. Water and wastewater service charges have also risen 3% a year since July 1, 2025 46.

How many jobs does Spaceport America support, by its August 2025 impact report?

790 in 2024, by the NMSU Arrowhead Center and C-BED report of August 2025, which gives 396 for 2019 and 985 for 2023 11. The report gives 313 direct jobs for 2024 11.

How full were Albuquerque hotels from January to July 2026?

Occupancy averaged 62.6% from January through July 2026, against 61.3% in the same months of 2025, and the average daily rate $116 against $112, on the STR data Visit Albuquerque publishes 12.

Is there housing in Los Alamos for the laboratory's new hires?

The press has documented a shortage. Los Alamos National Laboratory plans 1,000 to 1,400 hires, and about 900 of those are replacements for staff who leave, by the Los Alamos Reporter's account of a March 5, 2026 briefing to the county council 3; Los Alamos County permitted 15 units of housing in 2025 and numbered 19,407 people in the July 2025 estimate 52. The Santa Fe New Mexican reported interns camping in the forest near the laboratory in summer, with student housing a stated priority of the laboratory 53.
