North Dakota in Brief

Record gas and a record well count in June 2026, a bank the state owns, and six markets read county by county.

Feasibility Study Company works as a North Dakota feasibility study consultant for owners and developers and the lenders reviewing their loans. The owner or developer commissions the study; the bank, the SBA lender or the USDA Rural Development lender asked to finance the project is the one who decides on it. Each study is independent and built to a lender's standard. This page is a source record for North Dakota as of late October 2026, and it opens where the state's own numbers open, in the Williston Basin.

The Department of Mineral Resources' briefing of August 20, 2026, as the Minot Daily News reported it on August 25, put June 2026 crude production at 34.583 million barrels, an average of 1.153 million barrels per day, and natural gas at 3.58 billion cubic feet per day, the highest the state has recorded 1. Producing wells reached 19,961 the same month, another all-time high, and 3,040 wells were active on the Fort Berthold Reservation 1. These are secondary figures: the department's own site refused the connection when the research for this page was done, so every oil and gas number here is the newspaper's reading of the briefing, and a study that rests on one confirms it against the department's release.

The payroll series for later months reads differently. The Bureau of Labor Statistics put North Dakota's seasonally adjusted nonfarm jobs at 448.7 thousand for August 2026 and 447.3 thousand for September, the second a preliminary count 3. Its quarterly county file put insured employment at 420,702 in March 2026 and the first-quarter average weekly wage at $1,333 8, and the Census Bureau's estimate of the state's residents on July 1, 2025 was 799,358, as the Minot Daily News reported it 15.

Those two kinds of reading divide most North Dakota underwriting. A project in Williams or McKenzie County takes its customers from an oil field that set gas and well-count records in mid-2026. A project in Cass, Burleigh, Grand Forks or Ward County takes them from the county payrolls, airport traffic and housing markets set out in its own section further down. A study names which of the two its customers belong to before it uses either, and it dates the reading it uses.

Capital has a North Dakota feature of its own. The Bank of North Dakota, which the state owns, ended 2025 with total assets of $10.7 billion and reported a total return to the state of 4.69% for the year 2. Its buydown and participation programs sit beside the SBA, USDA and conventional channels a sponsor would use in any state, and the capital section sets the bank's results next to SBA's loan files for North Dakota projects.

Six markets have sections of their own: Fargo, Bismarck-Mandan, Grand Forks, Minot, Williston and Dickinson. Each is read from its North Dakota counties and cities only. Fargo and Grand Forks face Minnesota, and federal agencies draw both metro areas across the state line; the Minnesota side of each is left out.

Oil and Gas

June 2026 in the Williston Basin: a gas record and a well-count record.

The oil field is the first thing a lender outside the state asks about, and in the western counties it is the demand base itself. North Dakota's monthly production figures come from the Department of Mineral Resources, whose director presents them in a recurring briefing. For this page the briefing of August 20, 2026 is read through the Minot Daily News report of August 25 1, because the department's pages and the briefing's documents could not be opened.

For June 2026 the report gives crude oil production of 34.583 million barrels for the month, an average of 1.153 million barrels per day, and natural gas production of 3.58 billion cubic feet per day, which it calls a new all-time record. The previous gas record dated from September 2024, and May 2026 had run at 3.52 billion cubic feet per day 1. The count of producing wells, 19,961, was an all-time high as well, 129 more than in May 1.

Drilling is the leading edge of that output, and the same report tracks it. The state's rig count stood at 33 on August 19, 2026 and at 26 on July 13, and Baker Hughes's monthly average for July was 24 rigs 1. The report also lists drilling permits and well completions month by month, with 74 completions in July 2026 1. A study has to keep these series apart. Rigs and completion crews are a mobile workforce whose numbers move within weeks; producing wells and barrels describe the field after the crews have moved on. A lodging, RV park or workforce rental project in the west tests its occupancy against the rig and completion counts, and tests its longer-run case against production and the county payroll, never one in place of the other.

On the Fort Berthold Reservation, 3,040 wells were active in June 2026 and the report counted a single rig working there 1. The reservation spans several Bakken counties, so a site on or near it reads the reservation's activity and the county series together. Land status, tribal approvals and tribal taxes are outside this page's record and belong to the title and entitlement work.

Three inputs that a revenue projection tied to drilling would want were not found with a date for this page: an oil price, state production tax collections and the Energy Information Administration's state profile values. A study that needs them takes them from the issuing agency's own release for the month it models, not from a secondary summary, and states the price deck its projection assumes.

The county side of the oil economy is in the Williston section: covered jobs and wages for Williams and McKenzie counties from the first-quarter 2026 file, and the federal declarations that reached Williams and McKenzie in 2024 and 2026.

Banks and Capital

A bank the state owns, the commercial banks' 2025 summary and ten years of SBA files for North Dakota projects.

The Bank of North Dakota released its 2025 annual report on June 29, 2026. The release gives total assets of $10.7 billion at December 31, 2025 and a total return to the state of 4.69% for 2025, and it reports that net income rose from $200.4 million in 2024 2. Standard and Poor's kept the bank's A+ rating with a stable outlook in its 2025 review, and the bank reports a separate portfolio of programs that the Legislature directs it to run 2.

For a sponsor, the bank matters most as a partner to the lender making the loan. Its PACE and Flex PACE programs buy down the interest rate on loans made by participating lenders, and the Flex PACE page names business projects, child care and affordable housing among the uses it supports 16. That page carries no date, so its caps, rate reductions and participation shares are not printed here. A study that assumes a buydown cites the lender's commitment letter for the amount and the term, and runs its coverage test with and without it, because a buydown that is not approved leaves the full note rate in the debt service line.

Behind those programs stand the state's chartered banks. FDIC's summary for reporting year 2025 gives North Dakota commercial banks total assets of 59,309,013 thousand dollars and deposits of 51,007,223 thousand, with savings institutions holding 4,765,228 thousand in assets 17. The summary is read from FDIC's BankFind interface, which prints the reporting year but no release date, so these totals are given for scale and are not counted among this page's dated figures. County deposit totals from the 2025 Summary of Deposits appear in some market sections on the same terms.

The September figure will be revised in a later release, which is why a study that sizes demand on payrolls carries the release date with the number. Air Travel

Eight commercial airports: a record 2025, a softer first half of 2026 and a record September.

Air travel is one of the few North Dakota demand series published month by month. The North Dakota Aeronautics Commission reports boardings at the state's eight commercial airports, and its releases give a hotel, restaurant or meeting-space project a monthly reading that the state's annual tourism research does not.

Boardings set a record of 1,322,791 for 2025, as Inforum reported on January 23, 2026 from the Commission's annual figures 7. March 2025 had already set a monthly state record of 123,180 29, and the first half of 2025 came to 654,304 boardings 6. The first half of 2026 came to 642,823, which the Commission's release of July 20, 2026 described as 1.8 percent lower than the same half a year earlier 5. Each half-year value is printed here from its own release.

Then the autumn turned. September 2026 set a September record of 104,380 boardings and brought January to September to 973,044, in the release of October 21, 2026; the same release gives the month a load factor of 84 percent and 1,662 successful departures 4. A demand model that leaned on the first half of 2026 alone would understate the year; one that leaned on September alone would overstate it. The study prints both and states which months its occupancy ramp assumes.

Per-airport figures for Fargo and Bismarck are in their sections. The releases opened for this page do not itemize boardings for Minot, Williston, Grand Forks or Dickinson. They do record service changes: at Williston, Delta's service ended on September 7, 2026 and Sun Country began seasonal service on September 10 4, and at Minot, United added a fourth daily departure to Denver 5.

The Department of Commerce's tourism research for 2025, reproduced by Visit Beulah on June 4, 2026, described a dip in North Dakota tourism with early signs of momentum for 2026 30. Its visitor and spending totals are not printed here. A hotel study reads them from the department's report and sets them beside the airport months, since the two series measure different travelers: visitors who drove in, and passengers who flew.

Hazards

Four major disasters since late 2024, every one from wind, storms or fire, and the counties each declaration named.

FEMA declared four major disasters for North Dakota between December 2024 and September 2026, and two fire management declarations came before them 14. Read with the counties each one designated, they touch most of the markets on this page.

  • FM-5540 (Bear Den Fire) and FM-5541 (Elk Horn Fire), fire management assistance declarations of October 6, 2024 14.
  • DR-4852, declared December 24, 2024, for wildfires and straight-line winds on October 5 and 6, 2024: McKenzie and Williams counties, with Public Assistance and Hazard Mitigation 1413.
  • DR-4888, declared September 11, 2025, for the severe storm, tornadoes and straight-line winds of June 20 and 21, 2025: Public Assistance in Barnes, Burleigh, Cass, Eddy, Emmons, Foster, Grant, Griggs, Kidder, McLean, Morton, Oliver, Ransom, Sheridan, Sioux, Steele, Stutsman, Traill and Wells counties 13.
  • DR-4895, declared October 22, 2025, for severe storms, straight-line winds and tornadoes on August 7 and 8, 2025: Public Assistance in Barnes, Grand Forks, Griggs, Kidder, Nelson, Steele and Stutsman counties 13.
  • DR-4934, declared September 1, 2026, for severe storms, straight-line winds and tornadoes on June 7 to 9, 2026: Public Assistance in Bottineau, Burke, Divide, McLean, Mercer, Oliver and Williams counties 13.

Two readings follow for a lender. The first is the kind of event: every declaration on the list is for wind, storms, tornadoes or fire, and none is for flooding. The second is the program. The county designations under the three storm declarations are for Public Assistance; the research shows no Individual Assistance designation for them. A study for a project in Fargo, Bismarck, Mandan, Grand Forks or Williston therefore reads the property policy's wind and hail terms, deductibles included, as part of the operating budget and not as a footnote.

Ward and Stark counties, home to Minot and Dickinson, appear on none of the four lists. That is a statement about declarations, not about risk. Flood exposure is a parcel question this page's record does not answer: the research did not reach the state hazard mitigation plan, National Weather Service winter records, flood outlooks or National Flood Insurance Program policy counts. A study reads the site's flood map panel and the insurer's wind and hail rating for the parcel itself.

Costs and Insurance

Labor priced county by county, a levy that was recalculated in 2026, and building and insurance costs that need quotes.

The wage table in the economy section is the cost side's best dated evidence, and its spread is wide enough to change a project's answer. Bismarck's Burleigh County averaged $1,346 a week in the first quarter of 2026 and Mandan's Morton County $1,126; McKenzie County averaged $1,818 and Stark County $1,349 8. A staffing plan priced at the wrong county's figure carries the error into every year of the projection, and the debt service coverage test inherits it.

Property tax is the second line a lender checks. In October 2026 Bismarck approved a plan for the reimbursement it expects after Burleigh County miscalculated the 2025 mill levies used for 2026 taxes, as KFYR-TV reported on October 27, 2026 31. The amounts concern the city's budget, not an owner's bill, and are not printed here. The episode still tells a study how to treat the line: a levy set for one year can be recalculated after budgets are drawn, so the study cites the certified levy for the year it models and the assessor's value for the parcel, and does not carry a prior year's bill forward unchanged.

Three cost inputs have no dated North Dakota source in this page's research: construction cost indices, the insurance department's market reports, and the state's lodging, sales and corporate tax rates with their effective dates. Each is priced for the project instead. Construction comes from the contractor's bid or a published cost index dated to the bid month; insurance comes from the broker's quote for the parcel, written with the wind and hail exposure the hazards section describes; and a hotel or restaurant study confirms the sales and lodging tax rates in force in its city on the study date.

Energy is the fourth. Applied Digital's data center at Harwood takes its power through Cass County Electric Cooperative 32, a reminder that in rural North Dakota the serving utility may be a cooperative whose rates and line-extension terms differ from a city utility's. A study for any power-heavy use, from a cold storage building to a car wash, names the utility and attaches its rate schedule.

Rules by Asset Class

Data centers, ethanol, child care, senior care, lodging and fuel: what the record holds for each, and what a study must source.

Data centers and other large power users

Applied Digital announced its Polaris Forge 2 data center at Harwood, north of Fargo in Cass County, in August 2025; the North Dakota Monitor reported a first facility to operate in the second half of 2026 32. In October 2026 Harmony Township, also in Cass County, had before it a proposed moratorium on data centers, concerning Geronimo Power's Harmony Energy Park and its two data center buildings, and cancelled the special meeting it had set for October 22 33. In this record the deciding body for a large load in the countryside is the township. The study for a project of that size identifies the township, the status of any moratorium or zoning amendment on the study date, and the utility that will serve the load.

Ethanol and agricultural processing

Rough Rider Energy's ethanol plant near Glen Ullin was reported underway on October 27, 2026, with construction set for spring 2027 and operations for 2028 34. For a fuel stop, motel or rental project in the towns nearby, the plant brings two different populations: a construction workforce from 2027 and a permanent staff from 2028. A study carries the two dates as separate assumptions, because the crews on site in 2027 are not the people who stay.

Child care, affordable housing and small business

The Bank of North Dakota's Flex PACE program names child care and affordable housing among the projects whose interest it buys down 16. Licensing capacity and the state's child care rules were not part of this page's research. A child care study takes licensed capacity and staffing ratios from the state licensing office as of the study date and builds its demand from the county's count of young children, not from a statewide figure.

Senior care

Whether a North Dakota nursing or assisted living project needs a Certificate of Need or another need review was not established by the research for this page, so no rule is stated here. A senior housing study confirms licensing and any review with the state health agency before its market section is written.

Lodging and short-term rentals

No lodging tax rate, short-term rental rule or statewide hotel occupancy reading was found with a date. The dated demand evidence a hotel study can cite is the airport series in the air travel section and, in the west, the rig and completion counts in the oil and gas section; the study adds a competitive set survey of its own.

Fuel, truck stops and highways

Two U.S. Highway 85 projects in McKenzie County were to begin in 2026, as the Minot Daily News reported on April 18, 2026 35. Road work on a corridor can slow or divert traffic past a fuel site for whole construction seasons. A study for a truck stop or travel center on the route reads the Department of Transportation's schedule and traffic counts for the segment, which were not found for this page, and models the construction years apart from the years after.

Workforce housing

The same report describes McKenzie County's down payment assistance for homebuyers, run through its job development authority 35. A rental project in Watford City meets that program as competition for the same households, and a for-sale project meets it as support for its buyers; the study says which.

Fargo

Fargo and Cass County: record airport months, a county output figure and September listings.

This section reads Cass County and the city of Fargo only. BLS, FHFA and BEA publish Fargo metro figures that include Clay County, Minnesota, and the brokers' Fargo-Moorhead reports cover both states; none of those figures is printed here, and Moorhead is taken up on the Minnesota page.

Establishments in Cass County covered by unemployment insurance employed 126,659 people in March 2026, which the BLS release shows down 0.6% from March 2025 8. The same release gives the county's first-quarter average weekly wage, which a labor budget for a Fargo project takes from it directly. Cass County's real GDP for 2024 was 14,875,063 thousand chained 2017 dollars, from BEA's county series as FRED relays it 24.

Hector International Airport set an annual record of 590,542 boardings in 2025; the previous record, set in 2024, was 546,787, as Inforum reported from the Aeronautics Commission's figures 7. In 2026 it set a June record of 50,959 5 and a September record of 49,464, the prior September record being 46,258 in 2025 4. For a Fargo hotel the airport is the dated demand series to cite, and its records came in the same year the state's first-half total fell: a Fargo study reads Fargo's own months, not the state's.

Realtor.com's median listing price for single-family homes and condominiums in Cass County was $399,625 in September 2026, and the county had 712 active listings that month against 512 in May, as FRED printed both series on October 1, 2026 1039.

Cass County was among the nineteen counties designated under DR-4888 for the June 2025 storms 13, and the data center at Harwood and the moratorium question in Harmony Township are both in the county; both are set out in the rules section. What the record lacks for Fargo is long: the city's permit pages could not be opened, so permit values are missing, and broker industrial and office vacancy, hotel occupancy and room counts, traffic counts and the active lenders were not found for the North Dakota side alone. A Fargo study gathers them from the city, the county and the brokers' tables for submarkets inside the state.

Bismarck-Mandan

Bismarck and Mandan: two counties, airport records that passed 2019, and a metro output series BEA stopped.

This section reads Burleigh County, home to Bismarck, and Morton County, home to Mandan.

The two counties are one market for shoppers and very different for employers. In March 2026 Burleigh County's insured payroll held 57,385 jobs and Morton County's 12,010; first-quarter pay averaged $1,346 a week in Burleigh and $1,126 in Morton 8. A Mandan operator does not pay Bismarck wages, and a Bismarck operator does not draw only from Bismarck: a study for either names the county whose wage it uses and the counties its customers come from.

Burleigh County's real GDP for 2024 was 6,093,642 thousand chained 2017 dollars 26. The metro output series stops earlier: BEA's total GDP for the Bismarck metropolitan area was $9,430.711 million for 2023, and FRED marks the series discontinued, with a last update of December 4, 2024 40. That index is set at 100 in the first quarter of 1995 and the state index at 100 in the first quarter of 1980, so the two levels cannot be read against each other; each is read only against its own past.

Financing Programs

SBA, USDA, bank and EB-5 money for a North Dakota project, and where the Bank of North Dakota fits among them.

SBA's rules for a North Dakota loan are the national ones. The procedure in force is SOP 50 10 8.1 with Technical Policy Updates, whose effective date is October 1, 2026 48. Its standard 7(a) debt service coverage figure is 1.15x, and a 504 borrower puts in 10% as a rule, 15% if the company is a startup or the building special-purpose, and 20% where a startup occupies a special-purpose building 48. On studies the regulation reads: 13 CFR 120.160(b): SBA may require professional appraisals, a survey, or a feasibility study 49. North Dakota's ten years of SBA loan files sit in the capital section; readers who want the test-by-test method will find it at SBA 7(a) and 504.

USDA Rural Development's guarantees turn on where the site is. The research for this page did not find USDA's North Dakota announcements for 2025 or 2026, so none is cited. How a study meets the Business and Industry and Community Facilities requirements is on the USDA Rural Development page.

A conventional loan from a North Dakota bank may carry a Bank of North Dakota participation or a PACE buydown. Neither changes what the study has to show, which is that the project's cash flow covers the debt the lender sizes, at the rate the borrower will actually pay; both change the inputs, and the study runs the case with the program and without it. What a bank's credit committee reads in a study is the subject of conventional and institutional lending.

This page's research held no North Dakota EB-5 figures. The method is on the EB-5 page, with a research note on counting EB-5 jobs, and a sponsor looking for a North Dakota feasibility study company for an EB-5 raise can begin with both.

North Dakota Questions

Seven questions a North Dakota credit file tends to raise.

Who hires a North Dakota feasibility study consultant, and who reads the study?

The owner or developer commissions the study; the bank, SBA lender or USDA Rural Development lender asked to finance the project reads it and decides. Each study is independent, lender-grade work written for a bank's credit committee, for the SBA lender behind a 7(a) or 504 loan, for a USDA Rural Development guarantee and for an EB-5 offering. A North Dakota bank loan may carry a participation or PACE buydown from the Bank of North Dakota, which closed 2025 with total assets of $10.7 billion 2.

Is the June 2026 gas record enough to size occupancy for a Williston lodging or RV park project?

No. The Department of Mineral Resources' briefing of August 20, 2026, as the Minot Daily News reported it on August 25, gave June crude production of 34.583 million barrels, an average of 1.153 million barrels per day, natural gas at 3.58 billion cubic feet per day, an all-time record, and 19,961 producing wells, also an all-time high 1. Those are secondary figures, since the department's own site could not be opened for this page, and the study confirms them against the department's release. A lodging, RV park or workforce rental study ties its occupancy to the rig and completion counts in the same report month by month, and keeps barrels and well counts for the longer-run case.

Which airport months belong in a Fargo or Bismarck hotel market study after a record 2025 and a softer first half of 2026?

The airport's own months, not the state's. Hector International set an annual record of 590,542 boardings in 2025 7, a June record of 50,959 in 2026 5 and a September record of 49,464 4; Bismarck Municipal set an annual record of 309,533 in 2025 7, a March record of 29,343 in March 2025 29 and a September record of 25,218 in 2026 4. Statewide, the first half of 2026 came to 642,823 boardings 5 against 654,304 in the first half of 2025 6, before September 2026 set a September record of 104,380 at the eight commercial airports 4. A hotel study prints the months its occupancy ramp assumes and says which release each came from.

How much does the county wage change a labor budget in a feasibility study for Williston, Watford City or Mandan?

Enough to change the answer. BLS's first-quarter 2026 county file puts the average weekly wage at $1,767 in Williams County and $1,818 in McKenzie County, against $1,126 in Morton County and $1,333 for North Dakota as a whole, with 25,925 jobs covered by unemployment insurance in Williams County in March 2026 8. A hotel, restaurant or child care operator in Williston or Watford City budgets staff at its own county's figure, and one in Mandan does not pay the $1,346 that Bismarck's Burleigh County averaged 8.

Are the September 2026 listing prices in Burleigh, Cass, Grand Forks and Ward counties usable as values in a feasibility study?

As asking prices, not as values. Realtor.com's medians, as FRED printed them on October 1, 2026, were $499,923 in Burleigh County 9, $399,625 in Cass County 10, $407,183 in Grand Forks County 11 and $326,200 in Ward County 12. A for-sale or rental study tests each against recorded sales before it prices a unit, and reads the state's transaction series beside them: FHFA's all-transactions index for North Dakota stood at 539.91 in the second quarter of 2026, as FRED printed the series on August 25, 2026 36.

Does the insurance line in a feasibility study for a Williston, Fargo or Bismarck site have to answer for the 2024 to 2026 federal declarations?

Yes, through the property policy's wind and hail terms. FEMA declared DR-4934 on September 1, 2026 for the severe storms, straight-line winds and tornadoes of June 7 to 9, 2026, with Public Assistance for Bottineau, Burke, Divide, McLean, Mercer, Oliver and Williams counties 13; McKenzie and Williams counties were named earlier under DR-4852 for the wildfires and straight-line winds of October 2024 1413; and Cass, Burleigh and Morton were among the counties under DR-4888 for the June 2025 storms 13. Every declaration on this page is for wind, storms, tornadoes or fire, none for flooding, and the county designations are for Public Assistance. The study carries the policy's wind and hail deductibles in the operating budget and reads the site's flood map panel on its own.

Where does a retail or child care market study in Minot draw its trade area when the city's count held level?

Outside the city limits as well as inside them. Two Minot Daily News reports of the Census Bureau's July 1, 2025 estimates put Ward County at 68,233 residents and Ward, McHenry and Renville counties together at 75,694 15, and the city of Minot at 47,308 43. The new households are therefore beyond the city line, and the trade area is drawn to take them in.
