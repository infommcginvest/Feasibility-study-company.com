SBA in South Carolina

Columbia, a Charleston virtual office and Duncan, on county lines that split the I-95 belt.

SBA's South Carolina District assigns each county to one of three offices, and the assignment does not follow this page's market sections.

The South Carolina District serves the state from three offices. Power and Water

New gas generation is authorized at Canadys; a connection exemption where sewer capacity is short is still a bill.

A 2025 law for new gas generation, a large-load pipeline in one utility's report, and a sewer capacity bill at the statehouse.

The South Carolina Energy Security Act, Act 41 (H.3309), was signed on May 12, 2025 30. It authorizes Santee Cooper and Dominion to build joint gas generation at the former Canadys site in Colleton County and sets a fast-track process that runs to 2035 30. An intervenor letter filed with the Public Service Commission on August 14, 2026 cites Duke's semi-annual economic development report: 43 large-load projects, nearly 8 GW in all, in advanced development 31. The same letter states that Santee Cooper's experimental Rate L is mandatory for large loads 31. Those are load projects in one utility's report, not a statewide interconnection queue, which the research did not find.

Greenville-Anderson (Upstate)

A million residents in the Greenville-Anderson metro in 2025, with ReWa's Dig Greenville sewer tunnel program on its capital list.

The Upstate metro on I-85, where the utility fees step up through 2027.

The Post and Courier, reporting the Census estimates, put the Greenville-Anderson-Greer metro of Greenville, Anderson, Pickens and Laurens counties at 1,014,101 residents on July 1, 2025, 85,894 more than at the 2020 census 51. Nonfarm employment stood at 485,800 in June 2026, up 7,200 on a year earlier 5, and Greenville County alone held 302,812 jobs covered by unemployment insurance in March 2026 52.

For apartments the record runs five quarters. Cushman & Wakefield's series for the fourth quarter of 2024 through the fourth quarter of 2025 gives net absorption of 307 units, 662 units, 380 units, 422 units and 299 units, with vacancy lower at the end of the run than at its start 45. No asking rent or count of units under construction was found beside them. Industrial vacancy is published for the combined Greenville-Spartanburg market: Colliers measured 9.28% in the first quarter of 2025 53, a reading taken more than a year before the payroll figures above. Colliers names the Midtown I-385/I-85 submarket and the downtown business district as the office corridors 55.

A secondary fee guide gives a ReWa sewer new-account fee of $4,110 for a residential 3/4 inch meter through September 30, 2026, stepping up on October 1, 2026 and again to $6,947 from October 1, 2027, alongside a Greenville Water capacity fee of $2,780 and a City of Greenville sewer tap fee of $363 54. The guide's figures are for a residential meter. ReWa's capital improvements list also carries the Dig Greenville gravity sewer tunnel 56. The April 2026 winter storm declaration covered Greenville and Pickens counties 38.

Spartanburg

Fast growth on I-85 in Spartanburg County, and a warehouse market shared with Greenville.

Population and payrolls of its own; the industrial reading comes from the combined Upstate market.

QuickFacts puts Spartanburg County at 380,857 residents on July 1, 2025, against 327,997 at the 2020 census 57, and a television news report placed the metro third among the fastest-growing in the country for 2024 to 2025 58. Myrtle Beach-Conway (Grand Strand)

Room nights and rental listings measure Horry County, the Grand Strand.

Summer occupancy, a trade site's room nights and AirDNA's listings, without a full-year rate.

Horry County, the whole of the Myrtle Beach-Conway metro, counted 351,029 residents at the 2020 census 47, and the same television report placed it second among the fastest-growing metros in the country for 2024 to 2025 58. Nonfarm employment was 159,700 in June 2026 5.

Lodging is the best-measured part of this market. Visit Myrtle Beach reports average hotel occupancy of 70.2% for June to August 2025 70. A trade site puts 2025 at fewer than 6.5 million occupied hotel room nights and about $990 million of hotel revenue 71. Short-term rentals are a market of their own: AirDNA counts 20,613 active listings with an average daily rate of $278 and RevPAR of $151 for the twelve months to August 2026 72. The summer occupancy and the trade site's room nights stand without a full-year rate or RevPAR beside them.

Residential permits reach this page only as monthly figures from the Census series in FRED 74; the research did not retrieve a metro or county total from the Census annual file. North Myrtle Beach began requiring a current property survey with every permit application from September 1, 2025, by a contractor's summary of the city notice 73.

Hilton Head-Bluffton-Beaufort

Hilton Head Island's rental code rewrite of October 2025 and the I-95 widening through Jasper County.

Resort demand on Hilton Head Island, and Jasper County's growth, plant and interstate work inland.

QuickFacts puts Beaufort County at 204,433 on July 1, 2025 against 187,117 at the 2020 census 62. For Jasper County, the Post and Courier, reporting the Census estimates, gives 38,533 residents against 28,791 in 2020 and ranked it the fastest-growing county in the country for the one year of 2025 63. Nonfarm employment was 90,400 in July 2026 6.

Jasper County is where the infrastructure goes. Ferrovial broke ground on August 14, 2025 on the widening of I-95 through the county's first eight miles, an $825 million program of which $728.8 million is construction, due for completion in 2030 61. SCDOT describes the work from the Savannah River, on the Georgia line, to US 278, with Exits 3 and 5 being rebuilt 44. The TS Conductor plant, announced in March 2025 as the largest investment in Jasper County history, opened on May 13, 2026, and the SALT logistics site in the county is planned for a large buildout 64.

The Town of Hilton Head Island's council highlights for October 13, 2025 describe a rewrite of the short-term rental chapter of its town code, with no occupancy limits, a cap on exterior parking and a permit year from May 1 to April 30 65. The rewrite is recorded as passed on first reading on October 13, 2025 and, in the same research entry, as adopted in October 2025; the record does not settle whether a later reading followed 65. For 2026 the Town's release puts the permit fee at $150 per bedroom per year, in place of a flat fee 76. The research found no state law enabling or preempting local short-term rental rules.

Rural I-95 Corridor

Dillon, Marion and Orangeburg counties lost residents between 2020 and 2025; Colleton County's mega-site sits at Exit 57.

Six counties grouped for this page, where household-survey employment stands in for a payroll series.

This section groups Dillon, Marion, Clarendon, Orangeburg, Colleton and Hampton counties: a grouping made for this page, not one Census area. Population is falling where it is measured. Encyclopedia entries citing Census data give Dillon County 27,458 residents in 2025 against 28,292 in 2020 68 and Marion County 28,242 against 29,183 69, and a table citing the Vintage 2025 estimates gives Orangeburg County 83,177 against 84,223 47. No 2025 figures for Clarendon, Colleton or Hampton are in the record.

No payroll series covers these counties, so the only employment measure is the household survey, which counts residents with jobs wherever they work. In July 2026 it counted 11,722 employed residents in Dillon County, 10,874 in Marion County against 11,339 a year earlier, 33,652 in Orangeburg County against 33,174, and 15,573 in Colleton County against 15,298 6.

The investment is in sites and bridges. The Lake Marion bridges between Clarendon and Orangeburg counties are a $350 million project that won a $175 million federal grant on July 17, 2024 67. A Carolina Renewables Products plant with 155 jobs was among the 2025 announcements reported for Orangeburg County 66, and the Energy Security Act authorizes new gas generation at Canadys in Colleton County 30. Orangeburg County was in the request area of the January 2025 flood declaration 33. The research also looked for Inland Port Dillon's throughput and did not find it.

Worked Cases

South Carolina's hotel case is set on a coastal, interstate tourism corridor that its page places in no county or town.

A ground-up select-service property under SBA 504, from its competitive set to stabilized coverage.

The state's one published worked case is set, in its page's words, on a coastal, interstate tourism corridor in a fast-growing South Carolina coastal market, and the page does not place its subject in a county or town. The South Carolina limited-service hotel under SBA 504 takes a ground-up, select-service property from a competitive set defined by segment and location, through a ramp in occupancy and rate toward a penetration index, to the stabilized debt-service coverage its lender has to document. It sets its stabilized occupancy and rate within the band the state's coastal markets support, below Charleston's high-end strength and in line with the Grand Strand's rate structure.

No published case names a location in the Upstate, the Midlands, the Pee Dee or the six counties grouped as the I-95 corridor.

South Carolina Questions

South Carolina feasibility study questions.

How large were South Carolina's 2025 recruitment announcements?

The Department of Commerce announced $9.12 billion of capital investment from recruited projects in 2025, the third-highest year on record, with $4.25 billion of it in rural counties 7. The Governor's office had reported $8.19 billion and more than 5,500 new jobs for 2024 8. Both totals are announcements of investment, not counts of plants in operation.

What statewide figures does South Carolina have for an apartment study?

Survey counts rather than a count of competing space: the five-year American Community Survey's renter households, median gross rent and residents aged 65 and over, set out in the supply section. One statewide figure bears on apartment finance: $694,748,030 of the 2025 private activity bond ceiling was carried forward to SC Housing, with $219,748,030 applied to multifamily 16. Apartment absorption is published for Greenville and Columbia, by Cushman & Wakefield 45.

Which SBA office serves each South Carolina county?

Each of the South Carolina District's three offices has its own county list 13. Columbia serves Aiken, Allendale, Bamberg, Barnwell, Calhoun, Clarendon, Darlington, Dillon, Florence, Lee, Lexington, Marion, Marlboro, Orangeburg, Richland and Sumter. The Charleston virtual office serves Beaufort, Berkeley, Charleston, Colleton, Dorchester, Georgetown, Hampton, Horry, Jasper and Williamsburg. The Spartanburg (Duncan) office serves Abbeville, Anderson, Cherokee, Chester, Chesterfield, Edgefield, Fairfield, Greenville, Greenwood, Kershaw, Lancaster, Laurens, McCormick, Newberry, Oconee, Pickens, Saluda, Spartanburg, Union and York 13.

Which South Carolina counties are in the coastal wind pool?

Beaufort, Charleston, Colleton, Georgetown and Horry, on a secondary insurance site's list 28; the costs section gives the association's role and its 2026 rate change.

Why do South Carolina SBA lending totals disagree?

Because they are counted on different bases. One secondary source gives 970 loans for FY2025, 7(a) and 504 combined 10; another gives 927 7(a) loans for calendar 2025 in its header 11. The second source's own body text gives 827 businesses for the same calendar year as its header 11.

What happens to South Carolina hospital review on January 1, 2027?

It is scheduled to end. Act 20 of 2023 repealed most of the program, with hospital review set to sunset on January 1, 2027 and nursing homes staying under it 19. The Governor's release and the NCSL summary differ on home health: the release lists home health agencies as still covered, and NCSL lists only hospitals and nursing homes 1920.

How are South Carolina's markets grouped on these pages?

In three regions and one corridor. The Upstate holds Greenville-Anderson, a metro of 1,014,101 residents in 2025 51, and Spartanburg, which shares Greenville's industrial market 53. The Midlands and Pee Dee hold Columbia, a metro of 879,918 residents by an encyclopedia's reading of the Census estimates 40, and Florence. The coast holds Charleston-North Charleston, Myrtle Beach-Conway, where summer 2025 hotel occupancy averaged 70.2% 70, and Hilton Head-Bluffton-Beaufort, where Beaufort County held 204,433 residents on July 1, 2025 62. The rural I-95 corridor takes in six counties, where Dillon, Marion and Orangeburg held fewer residents in 2025 than in 2020 686947.
