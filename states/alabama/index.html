The Alabama Thesis

Migration drives Alabama's uneven growth by CBER's reading, and the international count is unsettled.

PARCA placed Alabama 15th among the states for numeric population growth in 2024 to 2025 and 20th for percentage growth; it placed the state 11th for its domestic migration rate and 42nd for its international migration rate 10.

Deaths outnumbered births statewide and in all but 17 of the 67 counties in 2025 2, and CBER at the University of Alabama names migration the primary driver of the state's uneven growth 9. The Census Bureau counted Alabama's net domestic migration at 23,358 for July 2024 to June 2025, above Florida's 69. The international reading is unsettled. CBER counts 8,946 net international migrants in 2024 to 2025 against 15,763 the year before 9. PARCA, reading the same Census vintage, describes a drop of 13,156 in international migration from 2024 and a fall of 1,595 in net domestic migration 10. The research leaves the two unreconciled.

The Redstone Arsenal and Eli Lilly records for Huntsville sit on its market page.

A memory care building in Alabama answers to Public Health, which requires a separate specialty care license, issued in group and congregate classes, to serve residents with dementia-level impairment 15, and beds converted from ordinary assisted living to specialty care have come before SHPDA's Certificate of Need Review Board on its recent agendas 29. The assisted living license beneath it is divided into family, group and congregate categories by resident count, from family homes of 2 to 3 residents to congregate facilities of 17 or more 30, and no new license is issued for a facility of fewer than three beds 31.

SHPDA's reach goes well beyond memory care. New institutional health services need a Certificate of Need from the agency 13, and the same recent agendas carried dialysis, ambulatory surgery, inpatient hospice and rehabilitation, a children's hospital relocation and substance use treatment 29. The statute's reach takes in nursing home beds, and the State Health Plan carries its own bed-need methods for inpatient psychiatric, inpatient rehabilitation and alcohol and drug treatment beds 13. The agency's revised program rules took effect on December 15, 2025 4. Earlier that month, press coverage of a Hoover health care project reported a moratorium on processing multi-specialty surgery center applications 14.

For fuel retail the state still backs cleanup. ADEM's trust fund reimburses eligible assessment and cleanup of gas station tank releases 36, it revised its contractor per diem rates from April 1, 2025, and it closed a new contractor qualification round at the end of 2025 37.

The Department of Human Resources posts a permit stage before a child care center receives its full license, and its application asks for background clearances and the director's qualifications 32; a secondary guide classes family homes at up to 6 children, group homes at 7 to 12 and centers above 12, each on a two-year license 33.

For bars and restaurants, a secondary guide reports no quota for most alcohol license types 35; a primary source on quota status was not found.

Auburn-Opelika's record holds a federal count of education jobs.

Birmingham-Hoover

Birmingham-Hoover: UAB, the VA and its CDCs around a shrinking city.

A seven-county metro with a payroll count to August 2026 and certified development company totals back to FY2010.

Growth runs to the edges

The seven-county metro held 1,197,766 residents on July 1, 2025, as Wikipedia relays the Census estimate 48. Inside it the direction depends on the county. Rural Black Belt

Six rural counties and Marengo: a broadband build and a health sciences school in Alabama's Black Belt.

Lowndes and Greene counties sit inside neighbouring metros.

The Black Belt is a band of rural counties, named here as Perry, Lowndes, Wilcox, Dallas, Greene and Bullock, with Marengo as context. It is not a census metro area, and two of its counties belong to neighbouring metros: Lowndes is part of the Montgomery metro and Greene part of the Tuscaloosa metro 48, and Greene lost residents in 2025 2. The research holds no aggregate population or list of largest employers for the region.

In March 2024 the state's commerce department announced a $230 million private project by Meridiam and Yellowhammer to reach 53,000 premises over more than 300 route miles in Selma, Demopolis and other cities of the Black Belt counties 54. The same center counts 19 of the 24 counties it places in the Black Belt in persistent poverty 73.

Alabama Questions

Alabama feasibility study questions.

How many people live in Alabama, and is the state still growing?

Yes, slowly: 5,193,088 residents on July 1, 2025 by the Census Bureau's estimate, against 5,025,437 in the April 2020 base 1. The city of Birmingham lost 4,333 residents between 2020 and 2025, and the city of Mobile lost residents during 2025 3.

What does the Alabama record say about coastal property insurance?

The Legislature has asked for a review: HJR220, adopted on April 24, 2025, recognised instability in the coastal commercial property market and recommended that the Department of Insurance review rate setting, claim denials and nonrenewals 12. The costs section sets the resolution beside the association that insures the coast.

Which Alabama health care projects go before SHPDA?

Any new institutional health service, which needs a Certificate of Need from SHPDA 13. The Review Board's recent agendas show the range: dialysis, ambulatory surgery, inpatient hospice and rehabilitation, substance use treatment and assisted living beds converted to specialty care 29. Multi-specialty surgery center applications met a moratorium on processing, reported in December 2025 14.

Which Alabama rules reach an SBA gas station purchase?

Two state tank rules sit beside the federal test. The Tank Trust Fund charge fell to $0.01 per gallon from the May 2024 return, down from $0.012 38, and ADEM's trust fund reimburses eligible assessment and cleanup 36. SBA's acquisition test, set out on our SBA practice page 67, is worked through in the Opelika fuel acquisition.

Who administers Alabama's State Small Business Credit Initiative money?

Innovate Alabama, through five programs of which collateral support is the largest, under an allocation of up to $97.9 million that Treasury approved in 2024 and that launched in June 2024 25. In the 2011 round ADECA received $31,301,498 for the state's capital access, loan guarantee and loan participation programs 26. The capital section sets both beside SBA's own loan file.

How much did visitors spend in Alabama in 2025, and what did lodging rentals on the coast take in?

The state tourism department reported a record $24.9 billion of visitor spending in 2025, up 4%, from 29.2 million visitors 8. On the coast, lodging rental spending in Gulf Shores, Orange Beach and Fort Morgan reached $923 million in 2025 58, and the Baldwin County section gives the 2026 lodging revenue and the named beach pipeline.
