Growth

Benton County's 10,553-resident year, inside a three-county metro.

The Census roster, the Vintage 2025 county counts and the components of Benton's gain.

The Census roster for the Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers metro is Benton, Madison and Washington counties, and that roster is the geography every register figure on this page describes 4. Reporting the Vintage 2025 estimates in April 2026, Talk Business & Politics put the metro at 622,177 residents on July 1, 2025 and 549,908 in 2020, and ranked its 2.4% rise from 2024 to 2025 ninth among U.S. metros, the first top-ten placing since at least 2020 by the article's headline 9. BLS removed McDonald County, Missouri, from the Northwest Arkansas metro in 2025, Talk Business & Politics noted in September 2026 10.

Benton County counted 332,554 residents in 2025 against 286,597 in 2020, and Washington County 271,213 against 246,756 9. The research carries no county estimate for Madison County, the third member of the roster, so a Madison County site takes its own county count from the Census release rather than a share of the metro.

The State Data Center's affiliate at UA Little Rock, in its Vintage 2025 components release, recorded Benton County's 10,553-resident rise from July 2024 to July 2025 as the largest of the state's 75 counties; net domestic migrants numbered 7,335 and net international migrants 1,591 1.

Our Arkansas page sets this market beside the state's seven other markets, each of which is a section there. The American Community Survey's five-year estimates for 2020 to 2024 count 215,566 households, and 76,861 residents were 65 or older 11.

Local Rules

Full sewer basins in Bentonville, impact fees in Rogers and Fayetteville, and a data center pause in Madison County.

Bentonville, Rogers and Fayetteville each set or proposed new water and sewer charges on new homes in 2025 and 2026.

In Bentonville four sewer basins had reached capacity, and the city had stopped approving developments that would feed them, the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette reported on December 4, 2025 2. On December 22 the council voted 7 to 1 for a one-time wastewater development fee on each new residential unit, effective in January 2026, to repay a loan of $239 million for the capacity program 3. The same December 4 report said developers cite sewer capacity limits in several Northwest Arkansas cities, not Bentonville alone 2.

Rogers Water Utilities replaced its access fees with impact fees on October 1, 2025: $2,400 for water and $4,000 for sewer on a 5/8-inch meter 12. Fayetteville's water and sewer impact fees, unchanged since 2009, stood at $1,843 per single-family unit and $1,311 per multifamily unit in May 2026, when the city proposed raising the combined charge by 208%, the Fayetteville Flyer reported 13. Whether that increase was adopted, and at what rate, is not in the research. Springdale's fees for new homes did not turn up at all. Bentonville's wastewater fee is a one-time charge, so it sits in a project's development budget, not in its operating costs.

Bentonville, Springdale, Bella Vista and Fayetteville each levy a 2% local advertising and promotion tax on hotels, motels and restaurants, in bnbcalc's 2026 guide, a secondary source built from Airbnb's collection list 14. No city short-term rental ordinance for the market turned up in the research.

Data centers reached the record as land use. Fayetteville adopted siting regulations for data centers in June 2026 instead of a moratorium, as Little Rock did, the Arkansas Advocate reported 15. Madison County voted 8 to 0 on August 17, 2026 for a three-year moratorium on data centers, with no project pending 16. Our Arkansas page dates the pauses other counties adopted in 2026.

Housing Pipeline

20,230 single-family lots, and the 2025 permit counts half-year by half-year.

The Skyline Report's single-family and multifamily counts for 2025, half-year by half-year.

The Skyline Report, which the University of Arkansas Center for Business and Economic Research publishes twice a year, with Arvest Bank on its second-half 2025 multifamily edition, is the market's only half-yearly census of lots, permits and apartments in the research. Its single-family count found 20,230 single-family lots in 341 active subdivisions in the second half of 2025, Axios Northwest Arkansas reported in March 2026 5. Residential building permits in Benton and Washington counties came to 2,929 in the first half of 2025 17 and 2,720 in the second half 8, by Talk Business & Politics' accounts of the two reports.

Multifamily permits are counted separately, by building and value. The University of Arkansas Center for Business and Economic Research recorded 144 permits for multifamily buildings, valued at $436.6 million across 25 complexes, in the first half of 2025 18, and 237 permits valued at $632.5 million across 27 complexes in the second half 19.

The three counts measure different stages. A lot in an active subdivision is land ready for a house, a residential permit is a house approved for construction, and a multifamily permit is a building, so a complex of several buildings draws several permits. None of the reports in the research counts completions or sales against them, and none of the three is a substitute for another in a demand model.

For the owner-occupied stock, the five-year survey gives a median value of $307,200 across the three counties for 2020 to 2024 11.

Supply

Apartment vacancy of 7.3% and warehouse vacancy of 9.7% in the first half of 2026.

Apartments by city, commercial space, the hotel record and the classes with no count.

Multifamily vacancy reached 7.3% in the first half of 2026, up from 3.7% a year earlier and 5.8% in the second half of 2025, in the Skyline Report as Talk Business & Politics described it in August 2026 20. Axios Northwest Arkansas reported an average asking rent of $1,145 a month for the first half of 2026, 4.7% above a year earlier, after $1,127.20 in the second half of 2025 6. Asking rents are what landlords advertise; the reports in the research give no figure net of concessions.

The second-half 2025 multifamily report counted more than 4,000 units under construction in Fayetteville, with 7,400 more announced, and more than 2,100 in Bentonville, with 4,100 announced; Springdale had more than 700 units under way, at 8.4% vacancy 19. The table gives each city as the report lists it; city vacancy in the second half of 2025 ran from 2.0% in Siloam Springs to 8.4% in Springdale 19. Oklahoma City's apartment pipeline and occupancy, by Colliers' count, are in the Oklahoma City section of our Oklahoma page, for a sponsor setting the two markets side by side.

Warehouse vacancy stood at 9.7% in the first half of 2026, retail vacancy held at 6.0%, and overall commercial vacancy was 7.6%, KNWA reported from the Arvest Bank Skyline Report in August 2026 21; Talk Business & Politics had put overall commercial vacancy at 6.3% for the second half of 2025 22. Refrigerated warehousing and storage counted 321 employees in the three counties in County Business Patterns for 2023 23.

The Census Bureau's five-year median gross rent for the three counties was $1,157 for 2020 to 2024, a figure for every renter household paying cash rent, old buildings and new 11.

No hotel occupancy, daily rate or revenue per room for the market was found, so new rooms are the only hotel record. The NWA Democrat-Gazette reported in February 2025 that the Compton, a hotel on the downtown Bentonville square, was set to open 24, and a secondary listing of new hotels recorded the AC Hotel Bentonville opening in May 2025 and Hotel Avail in Rogers, a Tapestry Collection property, in June 2025 25. A hotel study here builds its occupancy from its own competitive set. Self-storage, senior housing, RV parks and fuel stations have no Northwest Arkansas figure in the research, so a study of any of them counts its competitors by name.

Economic Base

309,400 payroll jobs in August 2026, and incomes in the federal accounts.

BLS payrolls for the metro, BEA and Census incomes for the three counties, and the employer gap.

Nonfarm employment in the metro was 309,400 in August 2026 against 302,600 in August 2025, both not seasonally adjusted, a gain of 6,800 jobs or 2.2%, Talk Business & Politics reported from BLS data on September 30, 2026 7.

Per capita personal income in Benton, Madison and Washington counties was $89,546 in 2024 in the Bureau of Economic Analysis accounts, a mean over residents that includes investment income and transfers 26. The five-year survey's median household income for 2020 to 2024 was $81,650 11. The two measures answer different questions: the first is total income per resident in one calendar year, the second the middle household's income pooled over five survey years.

The research holds no dated headcount for any Northwest Arkansas employer, so a demand case that leans on one employer's hiring cites that employer's own dated filing or release.

Lenders

SBA's loan files for the three counties, and two development companies that lend across Arkansas.

FOIA counts for Benton, Madison and Washington, the district office and the 504 lenders.

SBA's FOIA files, with loan status as of June 30, 2026, hold 769 loans under 7(a) for projects in Benton, Madison and Washington counties approved from fiscal 2016 through fiscal 2025, cancelled loans left out, totalling $423.6 million, and $58.8 million of 504 debentures approved over the same years 27. The 504 figure is the SBA and development company portion of each project, not its whole cost.

The market is served from SBA's Arkansas District Office in Little Rock 28. Two certified development companies lend under the 504 program across the state: Six Bridges Capital Corp., run by ACC Capital in Little Rock, whose coverage also takes in the Memphis and Texarkana metros 29, and Capital CDC, whose Arkansas expansion SBA approved 30. Where a lender asks for a study like this one, the rule it relies on is 13 CFR 120.160(b): SBA may require professional appraisals, a survey, or a feasibility study 31, and our SBA 7(a) and 504 page covers what the programs test.

Before Closing

Checks particular to Benton, Madison and Washington counties.

Each item rests on a figure or rule above.

  • The sewer basin. For a Bentonville residential site, the basin its lots drain to, whether the city has resumed approvals there, and the wastewater fee per unit on the application date 23.
  • The Rogers meter. The meter size behind the impact fee in a Rogers budget, since the published water and sewer amounts are set per meter 12.
  • The city pipeline. For an apartment subject, the count under construction in its own city in the second-half 2025 report, and how many of those units have opened since 19.
  • The lot position. For a for-sale subdivision, where the subject's lots stand among the 20,230 counted in the second half of 2025, and in which of the 341 subdivisions they compete 5.
  • The hotel and restaurant tax. For a lodging or food subject, the local advertising and promotion rate in its own city: the 2026 guide lists 2% for Bentonville, Springdale, Bella Vista and Fayetteville, and the research's list does not include Rogers 14.
  • The hotel competitive set. For a hotel, a competitive set built property by property: the market has no occupancy series in the research, and the openings in the supply section are its only dated hotel records 2425.
  • The traffic count. For a roadside use, an ARDOT count for the subject's frontage. The research retrieved no traffic count for any road in the three counties; the one interchange it names, Exit 69 on I-49 at Johnson, between Fayetteville and Springdale, comes from a secondary source without a count 32.
  • The county line for a large load. For a data center or other large-load use, whether the parcel sits in Madison County, under the three-year moratorium voted on August 17, 2026 16, or inside a city with its own siting code.
Northwest Arkansas Questions

Questions lenders ask about Northwest Arkansas.

Which Northwest Arkansas city regulates data centers instead of pausing them?

Fayetteville. It chose siting regulations for data centers in June 2026, not a moratorium, the Arkansas Advocate reported 15. Madison County went the other way, voting 8 to 0 for a three-year moratorium on August 17, 2026, with no project pending 16, so for a large-load parcel the county line decides which rule applies.

How does the Skyline Report count multifamily permits in Northwest Arkansas?

By building and by value, apart from the residential permit count. The report's publisher, the University of Arkansas's CBER, counted 144 multifamily permits in the first half of 2025, worth $436.6 million across 25 complexes 18, and 237 in the second half, worth $632.5 million across 27 complexes 19. Several permits can belong to one complex of several buildings, so the count is not a count of projects.

Are Fayetteville's 2009 water and sewer impact fees still in force?

They were in May 2026, at $1,843 for a single-family unit and $1,311 for a multifamily one, when the city proposed a combined rise of 208%, the Fayetteville Flyer reported 13; whether the increase was adopted is not in the research. Rogers moved first: on October 1, 2025 it replaced access fees with impact fees, water $2,400 and sewer $4,000 at a 5/8-inch meter 12.

Has apartment vacancy in Northwest Arkansas risen?

Yes. The Skyline Report put multifamily vacancy at 7.3% in the first half of 2026, after 5.8% in the second half of 2025 and 3.7% in the first half of 2025, as reported in August 2026 20. Asking rent still rose, to $1,145 a month in the first half of 2026 from $1,127.20 in the second half of 2025, 4.7% above a year earlier by Axios Northwest Arkansas's account 6; the reports give no rent net of concessions.

How vacant is Northwest Arkansas commercial space?

Warehouse space was 9.7% vacant in the first half of 2026, retail 6.0% and commercial space overall 7.6%, by the Arvest Bank Skyline Report as KNWA covered it 21; the overall figure for the second half of 2025 was 6.3% 22. Hotels have no such series. The record holds openings only: by a secondary listing, the AC Hotel Bentonville in May 2025 and Hotel Avail in Rogers in June 2025 25.

Did the Northwest Arkansas metro lose a county in 2025?

In BLS's reporting, yes: in 2025 BLS took McDonald County, Missouri, out of the metro it reports for Northwest Arkansas 10. The Census Bureau's roster is Benton, Madison and Washington counties 4, and on that roster the metro held 622,177 residents on July 1, 2025 against 549,908 in 2020, a 2.4% rise from 2024 to 2025 that ranked ninth among U.S. metros 9. Benton County counted 332,554 residents in 2025 and Washington County 271,213 9; the research holds no county estimate for Madison.

Fayetteville's water and sewer impact fees dated from 2009 when the city proposed raising them in May 2026.

A residential or multifamily budget in the city asks which schedule applies on the permit date, and the study prices each unit on that schedule rather than on the one it replaced.
