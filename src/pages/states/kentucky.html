KEDFA's loan participation and collateral support

Its incentives page lists credit enhancement programs under the Kentucky Small Business Credit Initiative name, aimed at loans just outside standard underwriting, and a separate Direct Loan Program of the authority, below-market fixed-asset loans for agribusiness, tourism, industrial and service projects, subject to the revolving fund's availability [^26].

For assisted living, the 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate counts 784,676 Kentuckians aged 65 and over [^21]. For child care, Type I staff ratios are set by age band: 1:5 for infants with a maximum group of 10, 1:6 for toddlers with 12, 1:10 at two to three years with 20, 1:12 at three to four with 24, 1:14 at four to five with 28 and 1:15 at five to seven with 30; for older school-age children the ratio is 1:20 over a full day, in groups of up to 30, and 1:25 before and after school [^30].
