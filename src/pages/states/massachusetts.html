Liquor licenses

Massachusetts caps on-premises and package licenses through a statutory quota tied to each municipality's population, and the statute does not lower a quota when population falls; Boston sits outside it under caps of its own [^39]. Short-term rentals

On September 8, 2026 the Governor's Executive Order 658 asked the Department of Public Utilities to have the distribution companies take measures to remove speculative data-center projects from their interconnection queues. MassDevelopment and the state's credit programs

The Secretary of the Commonwealth's business guide describes MassDevelopment as the state's main public financer of economic development projects: it issues tax-exempt bonds, took over MHEFA in 2010 and folded in the Massachusetts Growth Capital Corporation in 2025 [^75]. The state's SSBCI allocation of $168,591,178, announced on December 6, 2022, was directed through MassDevelopment, the Growth Capital Corporation and MassVentures [^76]. MassDevelopment's brochure for those products, which carries no date, requires a lender's own loan, from a bank or credit union, no smaller than the state's exposure, so the state money sits beside a bank loan and does not replace it [^77].

SBA lending

SBA serves the state from its Massachusetts District Office in Boston, with a Springfield branch for western Massachusetts [^78]. Eastern Bank reports that SBA ranked it the state's top 7(a) lender by number of loans for the 17th straight year, with 290 loans in FY2025 for approximately $43.5 million [^6]; in FY2024 it reported 305 7(a) loans and, as the top banking 504 lender, 23 loans under the 504 program [^79]. Rockland Trust reports that it was the state's top 504 third-party lender in FY2023, at $21.8 million [^80], and in FY2024 the top 7(a) lender by dollars [^28]. Official statewide SBA totals for FY2024 and FY2025 and the lenders ranked second to fifth were not found, nor were the three busiest certified development companies; the register's ten-year totals below come from SBA's loan-level files instead.

The federal rule on studies is 13 CFR 120.160(b): SBA may require professional appraisals, a survey, or a feasibility study [^81]. Loans submitted from October 1, 2026 follow SOP 50 10 8.1 with Technical Policy Updates [^82]. For the tests each SBA program applies, see the SBA 7(a) and 504 practice page; whether a lender should ask for a study at all is argued in the SBA feasibility study research note.

USDA Rural Development
