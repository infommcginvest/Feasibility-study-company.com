The Census Bureau's Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metro joins four Florida counties: Hillsborough, which holds the City of Tampa; Pinellas, the peninsula of St. Petersburg and Clearwater; Pasco to the north; and Hernando beyond it. The state's BEBR estimates, compiled by the Tallahassee-Leon Office of Economic Vitality, place the metro at 3,175,275 people in April 2020 and 3,403,788 in April 2025 [^3]. Both are April figures from the state's own series, a different calendar from the Census Bureau's July estimates.

The metro total hides a split across the water. Hillsborough started the decade with 1,459,773 residents at the April 1, 2020 census base that QuickFacts reports [^1]. Pinellas had 948,563 residents in July 2025, and Florida Trend's March 2026 summary of the Census estimates named it among the Florida counties whose population fell over the year [^2]. True North Managed's March 2026 rental update places new multifamily growth in Downtown Tampa, Pasco County and Southeast Tampa [^13], all on the Hillsborough side of the bay or north of it. A project in Riverview or Wesley Chapel can therefore point to arriving households; a project in Largo or Pinellas Park sells into a county that got smaller in the latest year.

The American Community Survey adds the age and wealth profile. Its 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate counts 672,215 residents aged 65 and over across the metro [^17], a base that matters for medical office, senior housing and the service trades that follow retirees. The same 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate puts median household income at $74,392 and the median owner-occupied home value at $345,100 [^17]. Insurance and the other statewide costs behind every Florida pro forma are on our Florida page.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics counts 1,545.4 thousand nonfarm jobs in the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metro in August 2025 and 1,559.6 thousand in August 2026 [^4]. The later reading is preliminary and is subject to the agency's annual revision before a loan made on it closes, and the agency does not mark the change between the two Augusts as statistically significant [^4].

Income in Tampa Bay is not only wages. The Bureau of Economic Analysis puts per capita personal income at $68,009 in calendar 2024 [^18], a measure that takes in pensions, investment income and transfers as well as pay. It is not interchangeable with the ACS household median, and a Tampa study names which of the two its demand model uses.

Tampa Bay splits the metro in two. Across the water, Pinellas and Hillsborough are joined by the Howard Frankland Bridge on I-275, the Gandy Bridge and the Courtney Campbell Causeway, so a three-mile radius drawn around a site in Westshore or the Gateway area of St. Petersburg takes in water and neighborhoods a customer would need a bridge to reach. A Tampa trade area is drawn by drive time at the hours that matter to the use, with bridge congestion in it.

East of Tampa, I-4 runs through Plant City toward Lakeland, and Cushman & Wakefield's Q2 2026 commentary, as relayed by FL Business Press Releases, names that stretch as a Tampa industrial corridor [^14]. The interstate continues into Polk County, covered in our Lakeland-Winter Haven section, and on to the Orlando market. A warehouse or truck-service site between the two metros competes for tenants with both, so the study has to say which metro's labor pool and rent benchmark it is using and why, rather than borrowing whichever reads better.

North and south, I-75 carries growth through eastern Hillsborough and into Pasco around Wesley Chapel, while the Veterans Expressway and the Suncoast Parkway open western Pasco and Hernando to Tampa commuters and US 19 serves the older coastal strip. To the south, I-75 leads toward the Cape Coral-Fort Myers market; to the north it reaches Gainesville. Port Tampa Bay and Tampa International Airport have no dated statistic in the research.

The bridge approaches on either shore, I-4 at Plant City, the I-75 interchanges at Wesley Chapel and US 19 through Pasco and Hernando are where a traffic count would settle a frontage deal, and the research returned no FDOT figure for any of them.

Multifamily: more delivered than leased

Permits first. QuickFacts reports 8,696 housing units permitted in Hillsborough County in 2025 [^1]. Across the metro, Acren counts 9,151 units permitted in buildings of two or more units and 8,771 in buildings of five or more units in 2025, a five-plus share of 38.56% of all permitted units, and five-plus permits 16.25% above 2024 [^5]. Apartment permitting rose in 2025, so the buildings now under construction already have a successor cohort behind them.

Performance second. Yardi Matrix reports an average asking rent of $1,783 and occupancy of 93.3% through May 2026, occupancy 140 basis points lower than a year earlier [^6]. MMG Real Estate Advisors reports an effective rent of $1,713 and occupancy of 91.4% for Q2 2026, with 12,857 units under construction, 8,262 units delivered over the last four quarters and 4,912 units absorbed over the same period [^7].

The 2020 to 2024 five-year American Community Survey estimate counts 426,933 renter households in the metro, paying a median gross rent of $1,628 [^17]. The lease-up and concession tests a lender applies are on our multifamily feasibility page.

Hotels: a hard comparison month

CoStar's November 2025 release put Tampa hotel occupancy at 63.3%, a decline of 21.1% from November 2024, with an average daily rate of $154.11 and RevPAR of $97.57, down 27.1% on the year [^8]. The same release notes that the comparison month, November 2024, was inflated by post-Hurricane Milton demand [^8]. County Business Patterns counts 454 establishments in the metro's hotel industry in 2023 (County Business Patterns), employing 14,262 employees in 2023 (County Business Patterns) [^16]. See our hotel feasibility page for the penetration method.

Self-storage: the pipeline is shrinking in the city

For the City of Tampa, StorageCafe counts 151,851 square feet expected in 2026, a pipeline it reports as 46.6% smaller than 2025's [^9]. Fewer new square feet arriving inside the city is the one storage trend the Tampa record shows; the suburban counties are not in it. Our self-storage page covers the competitor count.

Industrial

Matthews reports an average industrial asking rent of $12.86 per square foot in Q2 2026 [^10]. The I-4 east corridor toward Plant City and Lakeland [^14] is the one stretch the Q2 commentary singles out, and the same commentary reports that Lakeland took most of Tampa Bay's industrial leasing in the quarter [^14].

Unpriced classes

Nothing in the record prices Tampa retail, office, senior housing, RV parks or fuel stations, and no list of hotel rooms under construction came back either.

Around the bay the 2026 hotel tax splits in two: Hillsborough and Pinellas levy 6%, Pasco and Hernando 5%, in the state's table of local option rates [^21]. A Tampa Bay parcel's fees, zoning and capacity are confirmed through the written requests set out on our Florida page.

SBA lending in the metro is deep. The agency's loan-level FOIA data show 5,143 loans under the 7(a) program approved in fiscal years 2016 to 2025, net of cancellations, and 504 debenture volume of $760.8 million approved in fiscal years 2016 to 2025, net of cancellations, for borrowers in the metro [^15].

The SBA's South Florida District, the same district that serves Miami-Dade and Broward from its Miami office, maintains a Tampa-area office whose county list takes in Pasco, Hillsborough and Pinellas of this metro's four counties; Hernando is not on that list [^11], so a Hernando borrower should confirm which district office will handle the file. The governing rule is 13 CFR 120.160(b): SBA may require professional appraisals, a survey, or a feasibility study. For a new business, or for a limited or special purpose property, the 504 program sets equity at 15%, and 7(a) underwriting tests coverage against a 1.15x floor. Our SBA 7(a) and 504 practice page lists the tests behind those figures. [^19][^20]

Of the late-2025 C-PACE closings the Florida Development Finance Corporation lists, the Tampa Bay one sits in Pinellas, the county whose population fell: Sunday Beach Club, closed December 15, 2025, with no amount published [^12].
