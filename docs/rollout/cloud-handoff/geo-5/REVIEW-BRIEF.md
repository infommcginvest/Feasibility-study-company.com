## Attacker A: figures, dates, floors, research hygiene
Re-derive at least 30 counted figures per state page and 20 per metro page, weighted to secondary rows, rows the pre-count called borderline, register markers and anything the writer flagged. For each: the value on the page, its citation, the research row it comes from, and whether the cited source document carries that value for that period. Open the source URL (WebFetch) for at least 12 of them; a 403, 429, robots refusal or bot check is recorded, never worked around; never fetch www2.census.gov. Check: every printed number has a dated source (I77, I86); no arithmetic or comparison in words (I55, I59, I60); single-property values are nocount (I64); one vintage of a series per geography (ruling 10); register values equal `data/markets/series/<series>.json` values for their geo and none is null or flagged; source titles are the research's (I176); no figure is attributed to a source that does not carry it; the floors (A6: READY 30 in all with 20 research; landing 25 with 15) hold after your confirmed findings are applied.

## Attacker B: gates, links, sameness, buyer intent, claims
Check: the buyer-intent fields (title, H1, description, hero, opening paragraph, CTAs) say what the owner's rule asks and promise NO fee, price, turnaround, delivery time, guarantee, approval rate, office, local staff, site visit, licence or credential (no record states one); "SBA requires" wording (ruling 8); the FAQ and hero frames against the landed pages and the other GEO-5 drafts in `~/Downloads/fsc-rollout/drafts/geo-5/pages/` (I74, I81, I84, I88, the taken-frames list in `~/Downloads/fsc-rollout/drafts/geo-4/tools/SEO-BRIEF.md`); shared 8-word runs (beyond register labels, sba marker text and practice-area or hub names); required link edges (state and metro both ways; neighbours only where a fact makes the border matter); plain claims with no citation (name them; a factual claim with no source is a finding); keyword mentions (I89: three at most per form in visible text); the closing band carries no figure (I181); the gate outputs agree with the copy (rerun a gate yourself if you doubt it: `python3 scripts/qa/gates.py --root . --git-dir ~/Downloads/wt-rollout-geo-5 --urls <urls> --stage ready --only <n> --skip-look` from inside the copy).

## Findings file
Write `~/Downloads/fsc-rollout/drafts/geo-5/reviews/<t>-f1-<attacker1|attacker2>.md`. Each finding: an id (A1-1, A1-2... or A2-1...), severity (BLOCKER: a counted figure without a dated source, a figure its source does not carry, a forbidden claim; MAJOR-FACTUAL: a wrong statement of fact or rule; MAJOR-NONFACTUAL: frame or plan sameness; MINOR), the page, the exact quoted text, the reproduction (what you opened or ran and what it showed) and the smallest fix (prefer deletion, nocount wrapping or verbatim source restoration). Then your count of counted figures after your confirmed findings. Reply in under 200 words: the counts by severity and the page counts after them.

## Verifier (Sonnet, cold)
You get the two findings files. For every finding, reproduce it yourself from the frozen copy and the research; your default verdict is REFUTED until you reproduce it. Then re-derive the tranche's headline numbers from the copy: body words and counted figures per page (`python3 scripts/qa/pagecheck.py` or `scripts/qa/gates.py --only 3,4`), and confirm or refute each. Write `reviews/<t>-f1-verifier.md`: per finding CONFIRMED or REFUTED with your reproduction, and for each CONFIRMED finding the exact edit (old text, new text) as a Python list in `reviews/<t>-f1-verifier-fix.py` (`EDITS = [("<page source path relative to drafts/geo-5/pages>", "<old>", "<new>"), ...]`), deletions and nocount wraps preferred. A tally of attackers is not evidence: only your reproduction is.
