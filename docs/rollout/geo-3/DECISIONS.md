D15 (phrase census before every freeze). Ohio's attacker two found stock clauses carried between the lane's drafts and the live Texas page (the hero frame, the events lede, program-table cells, the verify lede) that the 8-word overlap gate and samecheck's section ratio both pass, because a long section dilutes a short clause. `tools/phrasecheck.py` lists every run of words a page shares with any other draft or served page, and every run repeated between two of its own sections (headings excluded, ruling 4). From t1's re-freeze on, a tranche is frozen only when its pages share no run of 8 or more words with any other page and repeat none between their own sections, figure frames included; the writer fixing review findings clears the census in the same pass. D17 (which page changes when two pages share a clause or an argument). Ohio's second attacker two round found 15 sameness majors, most of them clauses or argument shapes now shared with later drafts (Minnesota, Michigan, Kansas, Missouri, Nebraska, Indiana) that were rewritten at the same time. Rewriting the earlier page each round only moves the collision. Rule: a page under review fixes (a) any clause or argument it shares with a live served page (Texas and its metros, hubs, cases, notes), (b) any repetition inside itself, and (c) every false or unsourced claim. A clause or argument shared with a later tranche's draft is fixed in the later draft, which runs the phrase census (D15) against the earlier tranches' frozen sources before its own freeze; the collisions found against later drafts are copied into that draft's fix list (drafts/geo-3/<state>/review/carry.md). Headings may repeat across siblings (ruling 4); they are varied only where cheap. Generic lender practice (state the flood zone, get a written capacity date) is sameness only when worded alike or carried in the same order as another page's argument.

D18 (when a review round converges). Each round after the first narrows to what the last fixes touched, yet an attacker can always find a shorter shared run (Ohio's third round searched at 4 words). Ruling 17 requires every finding resolved by reproduction; it does not require rounds without end. A tranche is ready when its latest round on the latest freeze has no BLOCKER or MAJOR left unfixed, every finding that is a false, unsourced or wrongly sourced claim or a dropped qualifier is fixed, and every repetition finding is either fixed or recorded with its run length below the lane's 7-word census gate. The fixes of that last round are then re-frozen, every gate rerun, and the touched sentences re-checked by the verifier alone; if the verifier finds a new BLOCKER or MAJOR, another full round follows.
