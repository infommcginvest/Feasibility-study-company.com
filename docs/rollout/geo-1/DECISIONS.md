D1 (published dates of rebuilt states). Ruling 12 says to read a rebuilt page's published date from its served JSON-LD. The eight served state pages this lane rebuilds (Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Alabama, Oklahoma, Arkansas, Mississippi) carry `dateModified` 2026-10-10 and no `datePublished`. WEB-FSC-6 set the rebuilt Texas page's `published` to 2026-07-08, the day `states/texas/index.html` first entered git. The same rule here: `published` is the date of the commit that first added `states/<state>/index.html`. Florida, Georgia and North Carolina 2026-07-08; South Carolina and Alabama 2026-07-10; Oklahoma, Arkansas and Mississippi 2026-07-11. Tennessee, Kentucky and Louisiana are new: `"DEPLOY"`.

D2 (lane tools stay outside the repo). Ruling 1 bars builders from committing scripts. The lane's helpers (`collide.py`, which asks the WEB-FSC-6 page check whether a research value would count as unique against every other served page; `regdump.py`, which renders a geography's register values through `scripts/markets.py`'s own lookup and formatter) live in `~/Downloads/fsc-rollout/drafts/geo-1/tools/`. They read the repo and write nothing to it. No page figure is computed by them; a figure on a page is either a research value as written or a register value rendered by `markets.py`.

D3 (markets the research covers beyond the promised slugs). The lane line is "11 state pages and every metro that stands on its own evidence". A market an MT file covers that is not a promised slug of a rebuilt state is pre-counted like any other. It ships as a page only if it passes the pre-count and the gates; otherwise it is a section of the state page or a card on it, and no new slug is promised for it.

D4 (register figures, counting). Amendment A6 caps register figures at 10 counted per page and ruling 5 requires each to be a different series and geography pair. This lane reads "series" as the register series file (`cbp_estab`, `sba7a_loans` and so on): two classes of one series at one geography are one pair, so at most one of them is planned as counted. Research figures must reach 20 at READY on their own (A6).
