D14. Source dates. The readers applied different standards. The lane rule: a figure's source is dated if the research's citation gives a publication, filing, release or effective date; an edition year in the title; or, for a periodic document, its own reporting period (a 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026; a monthly report for August 2026), written as such in the source's `date`. A live web page whose citation gives no date of any kind is undated and its figures are not used. No date is ever supplied that the research does not give.
