D8 (case engine). Case pages render every model number (tables and prose values) from <!--case:--> regions. D9 (case engine). Pass and fail use the unrounded DSCR; DSCR displays truncated to two decimals, so a shown 1.15x always meets a 1.15x floor. Restructuring: equity to the tier, loan or debenture to its limit, then the smallest equity share (to 0.1%) that meets the floor in the test year (7(a) start-up and 504: Year 2 by default, from "start-ups reach 1.15x within two years"; USDA and HUD: Year 3 by default; Appendix 15 acquisitions: historical EBITDA). Break-even scales one driver on the restructured loan; the sensitivity matrix is DSCR in the stabilized year over two drivers, five steps each.
