D5 (factory). Source format: src/pages/<url path>.html with JSON front matter between lines of three hyphens; body is a sequence of <section id data-h2 ...> elements that build.py wraps in house markup with alternating bands. Citations are [^n] shorthand expanded per page type (sup.fn on state, metro, hub, subtype; a.cite on cases). Sources carry publisher, title, date and URL, all required by build.py. FAQ lives in front matter so the visible list and the FAQPage JSON-LD never drift.
D6 (factory). build.py keeps script-owned regions (chrome, mesh, fsc, sba, mkt, case) from the served page on re-render and writes their markers and params; mesh regions found in the served page are carried to the slot before the CTA band. --check compares served and fresh renders with every owned region blanked. build.py refuses em and en dashes outside owned regions, the ruling 10 names anywhere, a second <title> or <h1>, duplicate ids and JSON-LD that does not parse.
D7 (factory). Author JSON-LD is written in the exact one-line form byline.py's article_author() produces, so byline.py leaves build.py's output unchanged and both --check modes stay green. Dates: "DEPLOY" in front matter resolves to src/site.json deploy_date, set on deploy day.
D8 (case engine). Case pages render every model number (tables and prose values) from <!--case:--> regions. Market evidence tables (demand, competitor census) are source-cited facts written in the page source, not model output. SBA rules in case tables come from sba.json through casemodel.py (equity tier, DSCR floor and basis, size limits, FY2027 fees, Prime plus the maximum spread); SBA rules in prose go through sba: markers. USDA B&I and HUD 232 terms are case inputs cited to PA-USDA and PA-HUD-AGENCY sources on the page.
D9 (case engine). Pass and fail use the unrounded DSCR; DSCR displays truncated to two decimals, so a shown 1.15x always meets a 1.15x floor. Restructuring: equity to the tier, loan or debenture to its limit, then the smallest equity share (to 0.1%) that meets the floor in the test year (7(a) start-up and 504: Year 2 by default, from "start-ups reach 1.15x within two years"; USDA and HUD: Year 3 by default; Appendix 15 acquisitions: historical EBITDA). Break-even scales one driver on the restructured loan; the sensitivity matrix is DSCR in the stabilized year over two drivers, five steps each.
D10 (case engine). 7(a) rate defaults to Prime (sba.json rates.prime, H.15) plus the maximum spread for the loan size (rates.spreads_7a), the ceiling SBA allows, unless a case cites a lender rate. The FY2027 upfront guaranty fee is computed on the guaranteed share and financed (iterated to a fixed point); the rural waiver applies at or under $700,000 when the case cites the county's rural status. The 504 upfront fee (0.50%) is financed into the debenture. The 504 debenture rate is not in sba.json (WEB-FSC-2 dropped R48 as unverifiable) and not in the case research; each 504 case carries it as a labelled assumption or a cited figure, and pricing at funding is a lender condition.
D11 (gates tool). docs/web-fsc-6/tools/pagecheck.py measures body words (main minus tables, sources, FAQ, breadcrumb, mesh), unique sourced figures (cited blocks, outside sba/case/mkt regions, absent from every other served page) and 8-word shingle overlap against every served page. Run against 1d48cc2 it reproduces the charter's measured 56 to 66% overlap among the four gas station cases.
