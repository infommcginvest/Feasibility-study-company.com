 SBA Information Notice 
TO: All SBA Employees and Certified 
Development Companies 
SUBJECT: 504 Fees for Fiscal Year 2027 
CONTROL NO.: 5000-881796 
EFFECTIVE: October 1, 2026 
PUBLICATION: September 3, 2026 
Each year SBA reviews certain fees payable to SBA by 504 borrowers to determine if any of 
those fees need to be adjusted to cover the estimated subsidy costs of the 504 loan program. 
Fees are effective for the entire fiscal year to maintain the estimated costs of the program 
unless subsequent Congressional acts modify the program’s effective subsidy costs. 
This Notice announces the FY 2027 annual service fee (also known as the “on-going guaranty 
fee”) and upfront guaranty fee for 504 loans. A separate notice is being issued for the 7(a) loan 
program. 
For all 504 loans, the following fees are effective for loans approved October 1, 2026, 
through September 30, 2027: 
For 504 loans to manufacturers (NAICS Sectors 31-33), food supply chain (NAICS Codes 
1111, 1112, 1113, 1121, 1122, 1123, 1124, 1125, 1129, 1141, 1151, 1152, 423820, 4244, 4245, 
424910, 445110, 4842201, 4842301, 493120, 493130), and businesses located in a rural area, 
including loans made under the 504 Debt Refinance with Expansion option and the 504 Debt 
Refinance without Expansion Program, the upfront guaranty fee and the annual service fee 
will be waived. 
For all other  504 loans (other than loans made under the 504 Debt Refinance without 
Expansion Program) approved in FY 2027: 
1) The upfront guaranty fee will be 0.50% (50 basis points). 
2) The annual service fee will be 0.203% (20.3 basis points) of the outstanding balance of the 
loan. 
1 NAICS 484220 and 484230 are limited to refrigerated and frozen trucking, farm products, grain products, and 
livestock.

For 504  loans approved in FY 2027  under the 504 Debt Refinance without Expansion Program 
(other than loans to manufacturers):2
1) The upfront guaranty fee will be 0.50% (50 basis points). 
2) The annual service fee will be 0.203% plus 0.001%, for a total of 0.204% (20.3 basis 
points plus 0.1 basis points for a total of 20.4 basis points) of the outstanding balance of 
the loan.
