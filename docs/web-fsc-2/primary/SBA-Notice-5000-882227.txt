SUBJECT: Issuance of Technical Updates to SOP 
50 10 8.1  
CONTROL NO.: 5000-882227 
EFFECTIVE: October 1, 2026 
PUBLICATION: September 25, 2026 
The purpose of this Notice is to inform 7(a) Lenders, Certified Development Companies (CDCs), 
together known as “SBA Lenders,” SBA employees, and 7(a) and 504 Loan Program Applicants 
and Borrowers of the issuance of technical updates to Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) 50 10 
8.1, Lender and Development Company Loan Programs. 
The SOP 50 10 8.1, Lender and Development Company Loan Programs with Technical Policy 
Updates supersedes the version previously published but not yet effective. This updated version 
of the SOP is effective October 1, 2026, and will apply to all applications received1 by SBA on 
or after that date. SBA Lenders and SBA employees must continue to use SOP 50 10 8 for 7(a) 
and 504 applications submitted through September 30, 2026.  
In response to feedback received from stakeholders, SBA is providing additional guidance for 
clarity. Users are advised to fully read SOP 50 10 8.1 to ensure understanding of all 
changes. The principal changes are summarized below. 
SECTION A: CORE REQUIREMENTS FOR ALL 7(A) AND 504 LOANS 
Chapter 5: Other Core Requirements 
Trustor Guaranty requirements: When one or more trusts (revocable or irrevocable) own, in the 
aggregate, 20% or more of the applicant, each trust must provide an unlimited full guaranty. The 
trustee must execute the guaranty on behalf of the trust and provide the certifications required. In 
addition, when a trust guaranty is required, the Trustor must also personally guarantee the loan. 
All donors to the trust will be deemed to have Trustor status for eligibility purposes.

SECTION C: 504 LOAN PROGRAM SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS 
Chapter 1: Eligibility Through Submission of Application 
504 Third Party Lender Underwriting Submission: Updated the requirement for TPL’s 
underwriting analysis. The TPL may submit the underwriting analysis directly to SLPC. Direct 
upload features will be available in the future.  
504 Third Party Lender Loan Term: Removed the prohibition against the amortization of the 
Third Party loan exceeding the 504 debenture term.  
Collateral: Expanded guidance on 327 requirements for when an indemnification provision (non-
environmental) is present in the title records. 
APPENDICES 
Appendix 15: 7(a) Changes of Ownership 
7(a) Small & SBA Express Loans: The use of 7(a) Small and SBA Express loans for changes of 
ownership is permitted, subject to requirements in Appendix 15, and except for the following 
specific requirements applicable to 7(a) Small and SBA Express loans.  
• An internal business valuation is permitted when the Business Purchase Price is $350,000 
or less.  
• The loan must comply with all Appendix debt service coverage (DSC) requirements, 
including the applicable minimum DSC standard and required cash flow analysis. No 
reduced or alternative DSC standard applies solely because the loan is a 7(a) Small or 
SBA Express loan.  
• The loan must meet Appendix 15’s fully secured definition. However, unlike the general 
fully secured analysis, the lender is not required to include other held commercial real 
estate of the borrower or guarantors when determining if the loan is fully secured. 
Working Capital Adjustments (True-Up): Working-capital adjustments in the purchase and sale 
agreement are not treated as seller rebates and may be treated as cash to the Borrower. 
Special Purpose Properties: A “Special Purpose Property” is a limited-market property with a 
unique physical design, special construction materials, or a layout that restricts its utility to the 
specific use for which it was built. Change of Ownership transactions involving the acquisition 
of an owner-occupied Special Purpose Property and the business operating from that property 
may have a single loan with an amortization which exceeds 10-years, up to a 25-year term when 
the value of the property is substantially dependent on the operation of the acquired business. 
This exception only applies when the acquisition of the Special Purpose Property is integral to 
the acquisition and continued operation of the business.

Quality of Earnings: A Quality of Earnings report already prepared for the Applicant may be 
used if accompanied by a reliance letter or secondary review by a different firm. The report may 
not be prepared by or for the seller. For Cash Proof requirements, if the business has been 
operating for less than 2 years, the QoE report must use the information for all years in 
operation.  
Appendix 17: 7(a) Loan Maturity Requirements 
Mixed Purpose Loans: When 7(a) loan proceeds are used for multiple purposes (land and 
building, working capital, machinery and equipment), or the refinancing of any of these 
purposes, the maturity may be a blended maturity or, if 51% or more of the use of the 7(a) loan’s 
proceeds are for real estate, the maximum maturity may be up to 25 years. 
Farming Real Estate: When land and structures for farming comprise at least 51 percent of 
proceeds and the real-estate use includes construction or renovation, a reasonable construction or 
renovation period may be added to the 20-year maximum maturity. 
Appendix 19: 7(a) Collateral Requirements 
Reinserted language from SOP 50 10 8 related to collateral requirements.  
Appendix 20: Submission of Application for Guaranty 
Working Capital CAPLine Field Examination: The annual field examination requirement applies 
when a Working Capital CAPLine is $1,000,000 or greater.
