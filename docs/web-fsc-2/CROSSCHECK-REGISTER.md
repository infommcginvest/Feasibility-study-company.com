# Cross-check of REGISTER.csv (67 rows)

Files: primary/SOP-50-10-8.1-TU.txt ("8.1"), primary/SOP-50-10-8.txt ("8"), REDLINE-GENERATED-8-to-8.1TU.diff. Line numbers are extract line numbers in those files unless marked "diff".

## 1. Method
- Python script: normalised whitespace, quotes and dashes, lower-cased every verbatim_quote, and tested it as a substring of each primary/*.txt. Quotes that failed whole-string were split on "...", " / ", " | ", bullets and sentence ends; every fragment was then tested. A quote built from non-adjacent sentences joined without an ellipsis passes fragment-by-fragment (noted below).
- grep -n -F on distinctive fragments to confirm value_8_1 details that sit outside the quoted text (for example lines 1834, 5163, 5167, 5178, 5289, 5300, 3103-3124).
- sed -n on SOP-50-10-8.txt at each value_8_cite line (1629, 1691, 1768, 1900, 1937, 2033, 2076, 4671, 5053).
- Diff scan: parsed the diff, dropped every "-" paragraph that reappears verbatim as a "+" paragraph (E-Tran renamed), then paired the remaining paragraphs by difflib similarity and grepped them for the listed topics. The pairing run over the later SOP 8 sections (about diff line 2300 onward for 7(a) chapters) was not completed; the 504 chapter (diff lines 3500-4244) was scanned by direct listing. Treat section 4 as a strong but not exhaustive list.
- No quote in the 67 rows other than those in section 2 failed.

## 3. MAJOR findings
- **R14** (VERIFIED). value_8_1 says ESOP controlling-interest loans are exempt. The quote does not say that; its own heading reads "Business Expansion, Owner Buyouts, and ESOP & Cooperatives: 10%" (8.1 line 5173). The exemption is real but sits elsewhere: "Loans for the purpose of purchasing a controlling interest (at least 51 percent) in the employer for purposes of an ESOP are not subject to the SBA requirement for equity injection." (8.1 line 5178; also 5169). Cooperatives are not exempt. Add the line 5178 quote or the row misleads. The "measured on the purchase price" claim is supported at line 5167, also outside the quote.
- **R25 / R24 / R26 / R27 / R30** (VERIFIED/CHANGED/CONFLICT_RESOLVED). Parts of value_8_1 are not in the quoted text but are supported elsewhere: R25 "only the real estate portion may exceed 10 years, up to 25" (line 5126); R24 "lender must use the QoE earnings in the DSC" (line 5163); R26 fully-secured exception (line 5300); R27 "within one year" (line 1834); R30 projections fallback (lines 3457-3460). Quotes should be extended; values are correct.
- **R46** (VERIFIED). value_8 "Prior quarter 4.75 percent (not verified here)": the figure appears in no primary file. An unsourced number in the old-value column.
- **R38** (VERIFIED). "Up to $10 million combined" is the register's arithmetic. The notice (primary/SBA-Notice-5000-879058.txt) states a $5,000,000 aggregate 504 limit, $5,500,000 per project for small manufacturers/energy, a $16,500,000 energy aggregate, and the 7(a) limits of $5,000,000 gross and $3,750,000 guaranteed balance. It never says $10 million. Also omits the $16,500,000 energy aggregate.
- **R37** (VERIFIED). value_8_1 gives "CDC portfolio averages $95,000 / $150,000 in Special Geographic Areas" (8.1 lines 3123-3124). 8.1 also says, at lines 3103-3104 (diff +3703/+3704): "For Projects that are eligible under 13 CFR 120.862 ... a CDC's portfolio must reflect an average of one job opportunity for every $150,000" and SGA portfolios "may average not more than $150,000". The two passages differ and the register shows only one. SOP 8 (line 4673-4675) also had "75% of the jobs must be in the community" and job-retention conditions; 8.1 has 0 hits for both (see section 4).
- **R33** (VERIFIED). value_8 omits the SOP 8 sentence "For stock purchases, the loan maturity may be based on the underlying assets/interest financed by the 7(a) loan as supported by a business valuation/appraisal." (SOP 8 line 1768); 8.1 deletes it. A reader comparing versions would miss a change.
- **Status label inconsistency** (rows marked VERIFIED although value_8 differs from value_8_1): R09 (1.15 to tiered 1.15/1.25), R13/R15 (equity wording), R16, R17 (24 to 36 months), R19 (12 to 24 months), R20, R21 ($250,000 to $350,000), R22, R30 (1:1 to 1.15:1), R37 ($90,000 to $95,000), R39 (120 to 90 days for owner statements), R56, R57 ($90,000 to $95,000). - **R41** (CHANGED). Wording fuses two items: 8.1 line 521 says SBA Form 2268 (Debenture Closing Checklist) requires the Opinion of CDC Counsel, not that the opinion is "SBA Form 2268". Minor but a reader could mis-cite the form.

## 4. Diff changes not in the register (MAJOR)
1. **Debt-to-worth 9:1 and >90% financing conditions removed (change of ownership).** SOP 8 line 1939: "If the 7(a) loan will finance more than 90% of the purchase price of a partner buyout, both of the following must be met", with lines 1941-1945 "debt-to-worth ratio of no greater than 9:1 prior to the change in ownership" (same at 2448-2453 for 7(a) Small). Diff lines 1267, 1270, 1771, 1774 are the "-" text. 8.1 has 0 hits for "9:1" and none for "more than 90% of the purchase price".
2. **Business Expansion definition.** SOP 8 line 1938: same 6-digit NAICS, identical ownership, same geographic area, co-borrowers, "SBA will not require a minimum equity injection". 8.1 line 5067-5069: operating at least two full fiscal years with current ownership, buying 100% of another business in the same four-digit NAICS Industry Group, same or greater number of full guarantors. R08/R14 do not capture either test.
3. **Working capital line alongside a change of ownership (new).** 8.1 line 5289-5296: line of credit allowed; apply no less than 20% and not more than 50% of day-one availability to the initial balance; prohibited if availability would be under 20%. No row.
4. **Site visit.** SOP 8 (diff line 1088): site visit of the business being acquired for complete changes. 8.1 line 5053: site visit for the Applicant and the acquired business, with a virtual-meeting exception for non-physical businesses (diff +).
5. **Third Party Loan amortization before debenture funding.** SOP 8 line 4720: "...closed and begin amortizing prior to the debenture funding as long as the Third Party Lender obtains the Borrower's written consent." 8.1 line 3145: consent condition dropped. R35/R35a cover only the debenture-term prohibition.
6. **504 job rules.** SOP 8 line 4673: "75% of the jobs must be in the community where the project is located"; job-retention proof and 2-year anniversary reporting (diff lines 3698-3700). 8.1: 0 hits for "75% of the jobs" and "Job Retention may only be used".
7. **7(a) Small / SBA Express.** 8.1 line 1773 adds: "For Community Advantage Small Business Lending Companies (CA SBLCs), the maximum loan amount to any one Borrower is limited to $350,000." SOP 8 has no such text. 8.1 line 1763 notes the $350,000 internal valuation limit (R26 covers).
8. **Refinance/ESOP removals.** SOP 8 (diff 1028, 1474): "The 7(a) Lender may not solely refinance the Third Party Lender's loan for an existing 504 project" (8.1: 0 hits for "solely refinance the Third Party"); SOP 8 (diff 1004): "In a complete change of ownership situation, the option to assume the existing SBA debt should be offered to the buyer" (8.1: 0 hits). SOP 8 line 1705: cooperative controlling-interest loans "may be processed under PLP or SBA Express authority" (8.1: 0 hits).
9. **504 personal financial statement.** 8.1 lines 3969 and 3980 change 504 Form 413 age from 120 to 90 days (SOP 8, diff 3968). R39 mentions 504 Chapter 1; the 504 refinance/updated-statement mix (8.1 line 4032, "120 days (or 90 days for personal financial statements)") is not listed.

Checked and not changed: hotels/marinas/RV transient test (identical apart from apostrophes, R42); 504 Borrower contribution percentages (13 CFR 120.910, R31); 504 new-business 5% contribution review (present in both); special-purpose-property valuation by Certified General Real Property Appraiser (kept in 8.1 Appendix 15).
