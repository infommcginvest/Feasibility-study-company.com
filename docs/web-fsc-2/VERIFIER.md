Scope: SBA hub (practice-areas/sba-7a-504) and research note (research-notes/sop-50-10-8-1-...) checked against REGISTER.csv. Every figure, date and threshold I compared matched the register except the items below. No BLOCKER found.

## MAJOR

1. Research note, Equity section. "The same 10% applies to the other three categories, measured on the purchase price for an Owner Buyout, and there a lender may reduce or eliminate it when the balance sheet shows adequate liquidity and no negative net worth."
   Register R14: ESOP controlling-interest loans are exempt from the injection; the reduce/eliminate flexibility covers Business Expansion AND Owner Buyout, and also needs sufficient liquidity and working capital. The note states 10% for ESOP (wrong sense), and "there" narrows the flexibility to Owner Buyout only. MAJOR.

2. Hub, equity table, ESOP row. Cell "10%" next to "A loan to an ESOP buying a controlling interest carries no SBA injection requirement."
   R14: controlling-interest ESOP loans are exempt. The row contradicts itself and the 10% figure conflicts with the register. MAJOR.

3. Hub historical table (trust row, August column) and note historical table. "guaranty required at any trust ownership percentage" / "guaranty required at any trust ownership percentage".
   R40 resolution note: IN 5000-882227 is the only primary sign the August text differed, and the site "does not state the August 'any percentage' value as a verified figure". Both pages state it. Treat as an unverified figure. MAJOR.

4. Hub, 504 table, Third Party Lender row. "The bank's rate may not exceed 6% over New York Prime."
   R47: 6% over New York Prime "or the State maximum if lower". The lower-of condition is omitted. MAJOR (minor).

5. Hub, 504 table, Occupancy row. "51% of an existing building, 60% of new construction".
   R32: new construction is 60% occupied, with up to 20% permanently leased and a further 20% temporarily leased and occupied within 3 and 10 years. The lease-up conditions are omitted, so 60% reads as the whole rule. MAJOR (minor).

6. Note, Equity section. "a seller may stay on as a consultant for no more than 24 months in aggregate" (hub says the same).
   R19: 24 months in aggregate including extensions, for Business Expansion and Initial Acquisition. The scope limit and the "including extensions" condition are omitted on both pages. MAJOR (minor).

7. Note, Equity section. "Fees paid for ... are not equity; the buyer's out-of-pocket cost for the valuation and the quality-of-earnings report is."
   R16 says the cost of financial due diligence reports (business valuation, QoE) can count. "Is" states it as automatic, while the register says "can count toward the injection". Low-grade MAJOR; the hub has the same wording ("while the out-of-pocket cost of the business valuation and QoE is").
