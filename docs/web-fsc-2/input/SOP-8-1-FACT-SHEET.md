## What the live site gets wrong today (found October 10, 2026)

- `/practice-areas/sba-7a-504/` cites "SOP 50 10 8, effective June 1, 2025" in its stat block and sources. It has no 8.1, no DSCR floors, no Appendix 15, no loan limits and no fees.
- `/research-notes/sba-feasibility-myth/` calls SOP 50 10 8 the "current edition" and gives a 1.15:1 standard and a 504 equity build-up from an old lender example. It is dated July 2026.
- `/case-studies/gas-station-texas-sba/` cites SOP 50 10 8, calls 1.15x the "SBA floor", and gives a $250,000 going-concern appraisal threshold for changes of ownership. The $10M combined limit is already correct.
